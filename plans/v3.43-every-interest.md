# v3.43: every interest, new bridges

**Goal:** Race questions are set in every interest on the map, not just the strongest few. Each question links a goal to an interest it hasn't been bridged to yet, where one fits.

**Why (the admin, 2026-11-10):** questions should "fully leverage the MentalMap and suggest new bridge concepts".
- **Only the top 8 interests:** Claude is shown only the 8 strongest interests (`_loved`, by days talked about), and picks its own setting for each question. So the same two or three interests frame most questions, and interests talked about less rarely appear.
- **No bridges in the prompt:** the map's bridges (an interest linked to a goal in conversation) never reach the prompt.
- **Transfer:** reaching a goal from a second, different interest is the transfer idea in [learning concepts](../docs/learning-concepts.md) ("Analogical scaffolding and transfer").

This is the second of the admin's approved suggestions after v3.42 (fresher races), whose format and twist dice it extends.

## What changes

- **The server deals each question slot a goal and a setting (`race.py`):**
  - **The goal:** the picked goals are dealt to the 15 slots in turn, about 3 each, so the spread no longer depends on Claude. Due ideas' goals come first.
  - **The setting:** an interest group from all the student's active interests. The kinds under a general interest count with it, so a group with five kinds doesn't get five times the turns. Within a group, one of its names is drawn.
    - Groups whose settings were used least in the last 30 days come first.
    - For each slot, a group with **no bridge to that slot's goal** comes before one that has one.
    - Set-aside interests are never dealt, and neither are names a goal doesn't win over (`race_view`).
  - **Each slot gets two settings:** the dealt one and a second choice. Claude may use the second when the first would make a forced question ("volcanoes and verbs").
- **The question plan line** grows from format and twist to goal, setting, format and twist: `1. Fractions, set in trains (or baking): odd one out, a record`.
- **Claude says what it used:** each question returns its `setting`, a new string in `QUIZ_SCHEMA`.
  - **The check:** `check_questions` keeps it only when it names one of the map's interests, and otherwise leaves it blank. The question still races.
- **What's kept (labels only, rule 6):**
  - the setting goes in the results log beside the format;
  - each idea in the concept ledger keeps the setting it was asked in, which feeds "least used" (`ConceptStore.settings_since`);
  - `stats.sqlite` gains a `setting` column;
  - interest names are already on disk in the map, so nothing new is stored.
- **The Interests line** (the top 8 with kinds) stays as background for Claude. A goal's line also lists the interests already bridged to it: `- Fractions (level 4; bridged from: pizza; asked lately: …)`.
- **Sameness numbers:**
  - the log line gains `N settings`;
  - the Variety line in `lerni stats USER` gains `settings: trains 4, baking 3, …`, most used first;
  - the admin reference says how to read both.
- **Clean-up from v3.42:** `ConceptStore.recent` and its 40-label cap are only used by tests now. They're removed, and the tests read `asked_by_goal`.
- **Docs drift since v3.41, fixed here:**
  - the morning write-ahead in ARCHITECTURE and spec 09;
  - the one-box-back rule in spec 09b;
  - "asked lately" in spec 09;
  - the upcoming versions reordered by the admin (2026-11-10): this version, then proposals from races, then away from home.

No screen changes, so there's no mockup. Races look the same; the questions are set in more of the student's interests.

## Rules

- **Rule 5:** a race never records a bridge. Links come only from the tagger reading the conversation, as today. The race only prefers interests without one.
- **Rule 6:** settings are interest names, already on the map. No question text.

## Tests (minimal, fakes only)

- **`tests/student/test_fresher_races.py`, the happy path:** a map with 6 interests (one with two kinds), 2 goals, and one interest bridged to Fractions.
  - The question plan deals both goals and gives every slot a setting.
  - The interest used most in the last 30 days comes last.
  - The interest bridged to Fractions is never a Fractions slot's first setting while another interest is free.
  - The two kinds count as one group.
  - The setting Claude returns reaches the concept ledger.
- **Safety, in the same file:** a set-aside interest is never dealt as a setting, even when it's least used.
- **`tests/student/test_stats.py`:** the `setting` column and the Variety line's settings, in one assert.

**The eval (real calls, by hand):** `scripts/eval_race.py` gains one case: with 6 interests, a set uses at least 4 settings, and most questions keep the slot's goal.

## Deploying

- **Safe on the home server while in use:** pull, reinstall, restart. A restart clears open conversations, and the morning write-ahead runs again at start-up (about 3 minutes).
- **Nothing to set up.** Older ledger entries have no setting and count as unused. `stats.sqlite` gains its `setting` column at the next rebuild.
