# v3.16 The race looks and sounds better: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The race is won by answers, against real cars, on a bigger track, with sound:
- **Rivals:** two garage cars, paced from the student's own car, so 4 or 5 right wins in any car.
- **Sounds:** a roar for a right answer, a sputter for a wrong one, a finish sound, and 🔇.
- **From Games:**
  - the white car is no longer faint;
  - the MentalMap picture's labels are readable in dark mode;
  - the slowed rival named is never one behind.

**Architecture:**
- **Pacing (`race.py`):**
  - **What goes away:** `BOTS` (the bots' steady steps).
  - **The new type:** `Rival(name, car, step)`, a frozen dataclass.
  - **The pace:** `pace(boost) -> (fast, slow)` gives each rival's per-turn step. It's derived from the student's own finish `S(k) = STEP_RIGHT·b·k + STEP_WRONG·(5 − k)`:
    - `_between(k, b)` is the midpoint of `S(k) + k·SLOW` and `S(k+1) + (k+1)·SLOW`, divided by 5;
    - the fast rival is `_between(3, b)`, and the slow one `_between(2, b)`.

    The constants stay, and nothing new is tuned.
  - **Starting a race:** `new_race(questions, boost, car, rivals=BOT_RIVALS)` takes two `(name, car_id)` pairs, fast first, and sets their steps from `pace(boost)`.
  - **Positions:** `Race.bots` becomes `Race.rivals` (a tuple of `Rival`) and `Race.at` (their positions).
  - **The slowed name:** `Turn.slowed` names the nearest rival ahead, or is `""` when none is ahead. The line then says "Both slowed down 💨".
- **Picking rivals (`garage.py`):** `Garage.rivals(owned, driving, balance) -> tuple[Car | None, Car | None]`, the cars for the fast and slow lanes, never the driven car and never the same car twice.
  - **The fast lane:**
    1. the car `next_goal` names (the one they're saving for);
    2. if every car is owned, their fastest other owned car;
    3. otherwise Zippy.
  - **The slow lane:**
    1. their fastest owned car with a boost at or below the driven car's;
    2. otherwise Bolt.
  - **In the race:** `games.py` turns the picks into `(name, id)` pairs, with Zippy and Bolt filling a `None`, and passes them through `RaceDesk.start(…, rivals=…)` to `new_race`.
- **The track (`web/games.py`, `garage.py`):**
  - **Width:** `START` moves from 40 to 8, so the cars use the whole width.
  - **Labels:** each car's name is a small label (font size 8) at the top-left of its lane, on a light pill, at most 18 characters (with "…").
  - **Outlines:** every car gets an SVG `feDropShadow` edge (`#2C2C2A`, stdDeviation 0.7), so a white car shows on the light track.
  - **Rival cars** are drawn by `Garage.svg`: a picture for a real car, a drawing for a cartoon one.
  - **Sound marks:** the SVG carries `data-step` (answers so far) and `data-sound` (`roar`, `sputter`, `finish`, or empty) for `race.js`.
- **Map labels in dark mode (`web/mapdraw.py`):** the item names use `fill="currentColor"` instead of `#222`, so they follow the theme's text color.
- **Sounds:**
  - **The route (`web/app.py`):** `GET /sounds/{name}` serves `<data>/sounds/<name>.mp3` only for `name` in `SOUNDS = ("roar", "sputter", "finish")`, only to someone signed in. Otherwise it returns 404, like `/cars/`.
  - **Playback (`race.js`):**
    - **Unlocking:** the first Race! tap (`#lerni-go`) creates and resumes an `AudioContext`, then fetches and decodes the three clips. A failed fetch leaves that clip silent.
    - **When:** when the track's `data-step` changes for the same race, it plays `data-sound`, at most once per race and step.
    - **How long:** each clip plays from its start for 1.5 s (the finish, 2.5 s), with a 0.3 s fade-out through a `GainNode`, at 0.6 volume.
  - **🔇:**
    - a static `gr.HTML` button, `#lerni-mute`, shown with the track;
    - `race.js` handles taps by delegation, saves `lerni-sound` = `off`/`on` in `localStorage` (in a try/catch), and refreshes the button's label on every redraw.
- **The clips (on the home server only, never committed):** `~/.lerni/student/sounds/` and `~/.lerni/student-dev/sounds/`, copied from the admin's downloads:
  - `roar.mp3` ← `dragon-studio-car-engine-roaring-376881.mp3`;
  - `sputter.mp3` ← `universfield-car-engine-failure-352768.mp3`;
  - `finish.mp3` ← `chrysalyn-sports-car-acceleration-and-driving-away-562433.mp3`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+, Gradio 6.30 (existing components, no `gr.render`), vanilla JS (Web Audio, no build), pytest.

- **Rule 6:** `localStorage` holds only `lerni-sound`, never text.
- **No new dependency,** and no audio tool. The clips aren't trimmed; `race.js` cuts them short.
- **Tests:** one test per safety guarantee or behavior change, in existing files:

     - a right answer when the student already leads names no rival.
  2. `test_garage.py`, `test_rivals_are_the_next_car_and_an_owned_slower_one`:
     - with real cars, the fast lane is the car `next_goal` names, and the slow lane is an owned slower car;
     - driving the starter with nothing else owned, the picks are `(car they save for, None)`, so Bolt drives the slow lane;
     - in an empty cartoon garage, the bots.
  3. The existing picture-route test also covers `/sounds/`: it refuses someone signed out and a name not in `SOUNDS`.
- **Screen check:** at phone size, on a throwaway localhost server with a generated test account, deleted afterwards:
  - a race to the end with sound and 🔇 (CLAUDE.md rule 9);
  - the MentalMap picture in dark mode, as an educator with both lists, right after a restart.

## Review Focus

1. **A rival is the driven car, or both lanes are the same car:** never. Check it with only the starter owned and with every car owned.
2. **A garage file edited mid-race** (a rival's car gone): the race keeps its rivals' names, and `Garage.svg` falls back to a drawing; no crash.
3. **Sound replays:** a redraw from a repeat tap, a page refresh, or switching tabs never plays a clip twice for the same race and step. A new race plays from step 1.
4. **iPad Safari:** sounds start only after the Race! tap, and the ringer switch may mute Web Audio (accepted; the admin checks on the iPad).
5. **Long rival names** ("Mercedes-AMG ONE Edition …") stay within their lane at phone width.
