# v4.17: new arrivals

**Goal:** a new car every week, chosen by the student's ❤️, so there's always something coming ([spec 09c](specs/09c-garage.md), [upcoming versions](../docs/todo.md#upcoming-versions)). No Claude calls.

## The admin's calls (2026-11-19)

- **New arrivals before check a few questions,** which becomes v4.18.
- **A weekly slot, ❤️ picks:** the admin loads the covered cars once. One arrives each week on a set weekday. The student's hearts choose which car comes, ties going to the cheapest, so an affordable one keeps coming. No deploys are needed during the trip.
- **Cars from Wikimedia Commons:** free-licence side-on photos (the admin approves each download) and drafted stats with a source per number, checked by the admin. Each photo's credit is kept in the private garage file.
- **With this PR:** three README badges (CI, version from the latest tag, MIT) and CI on pushes to `main`.

## My calls

- **`"coming": true` on a car in `garage.json`,** in place of the spec's `arrives` date per car, which the weekly slot replaces. A file without it works as now (tolerant reader); a car without it is already here.
- **The delivery day** is `ARRIVAL_WEEKDAY` (Friday) in `schedule.py`, beside the other job times.
- **Per student, in their points file:**
  - `hearts`: the car ids they want;
  - `arrived`: car id → the day it arrived;
  - `delivery`: the next delivery day.
  
  Ids and days only.
- **How a delivery works:**
  - The first delivery day is the first Friday after the student first sees a covered car. Once shown, a delivery day never moves.
  - **On or after that day,** the next time Games opens, one car arrives, dated that day: the cheapest hearted car, or the cheapest covered car when none is hearted. The next delivery is the first Friday after today.
  - **Weeks away don't pile up:** at most one car waits for the student, so the collection lasts.
- **Feedback that informs:** the car that would come if the delivery were now shows "Arrives Friday". The others show "❤️ to pick". A heart moves the label at once.
- **An arrived car joins the Showroom** at its real price (dollars ÷ 2,500 ⭐). It still has to be bought with ⭐.
- **Never bought before it arrives:** `Garage.sold` leaves out covered cars that haven't arrived, and every caller passes the student's arrivals. That covers buying (`garage_tap`), the next goal, the rivals and the facts race questions use. The heart button can only heart a covered car.
- **The educator** sees one more chip on the student's progress: the next delivery and the hearted cars.
- **Not in this version:** a "New!" tag, a delivery moment, or a count of cars (v4.19 brings the garage as a place).

## Code

- **`schedule.py`:** `ARRIVAL_WEEKDAY`.
- **`garage.py`:**
  - `Car.coming`;
  - `Garage.coming` (the covered cars);
  - `sold(owned, arrived)` and `in_reach(…, arrived)`;
  - `rivals` passes the arrivals on;
  - `deliver(coming, hearts, arrived, delivery, today) -> (car arriving or None, the delivery day)`, a pure function;
  - `next_pick(…)`;
  - `arrival_label(day, today)`.
- **`points.py`:**
  - `Points.hearts`, `arrived` and `delivery`, read tolerantly;
  - `heart(username, car_id)`, a toggle;
  - `settle(username, garage, today)`, which runs `deliver` and saves.
- **`web/games.py`:**
  - settle before drawing Games and in `garage_tap`;
  - `garage_tap` hearts a covered car and buys an arrived one;
  - the Arriving section, built once, one tile per covered car, like the Showroom;
  - `covered_html`;
  - the educator chip.
- **`race.py`:** `in_reach` with the arrivals.
- **README:** the badges. **`.github/workflows/check.yml`:** `push: branches: [main]`.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **Happy path:**
  - a covered car shows its day;
  - a heart picks it over a cheaper one;
  - on the day, it arrives and can be bought;
  - the next delivery is a week on.
- **The safety guarantee:** `garage_tap` on a covered car never buys it, even with ⭐ to spare. It hearts it.
