# v4.6.5: a home-screen icon

**Goal:** a Lerni bookmark on a phone or iPad shows Lerni's own icon instead of a plain gray letter, and production and development look different.

## Why (the admin, 2026-11-16)

- Bookmarks for the Tailscale addresses sat beside Kid Points' icons as gray "L"s; Lerni gave the phone no icon.

## The admin's calls

- **The MentalMap icon** (from a mockup of three): four interests linked to a golden L.
- **Production:** green. **Development:** gray with a red DEV band, as Kid Points does.

## Code

- **`scripts/make_icons.py`:** draws both 180 × 180 icons with Pillow, run by hand; the PNGs are committed beside the scripts in `web/` and packaged.
- **`web/signin_page.py`:** `/apple-touch-icon.png` serves the icon for this server (development's when it has a label) to anyone, since the phone fetches it signed out; `icon_head(label)` adds the icon tags and the bookmark's name (Lerni, or Lerni Dev).
- **`web/app.py`:** the app page's head gets the same tags.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- The icon loads signed out as a PNG, the sign-in page points to it, and development serves its own.

## Checks

- On the phone: a new bookmark of each address shows its icon and name. Old bookmarks keep the gray letter until removed and added again.

## Deploying

- **Safe while in use:** pull, reinstall (the icons are package files), restart. A restart clears open conversations.
- **Then** remove and re-add each Lerni bookmark once.
