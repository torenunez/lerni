# v3.36: probes

**Goal:** Now and then a race question tries a nearby idea that isn't on the map yet ("earthquakes" next to volcanoes). After the race, up to two "Want to find out more?" buttons open Chat on those ideas. What the student gets right untaught, and what they choose to explore, is kept as labels for Version 5's suggested goals.

**Spec:** [spec 09b](specs/09b-harder-races.md), "Probes" and "Want to find out more?"; the admin's decisions of 2026-10-19 (probe results feed Version 5, never the map directly) and 2026-10-30 (probes come first). Refreshed from the v3.26 plan, which was written before questions were held ahead (v3.27), goals had ids (v3.33), and the bonus took question 4 (v3.31).

## Changes

### 1. Writing (`race.py`)

- **`nearby(m, today)`:** the sources, as names:
  - the strong interests (top-level groups with up to 3 kinds each, as `quiz_input` lists them);
  - goals explained in the last 30 days.
- **`quiz_input`** gains two lines, only when `nearby` has something:
  - "Nearby ideas to try (one step beyond these, never on their map): …";
  - "Recently probed, choose others: …" (the last 30 days, from the results log).
- **`QUIZ_SCHEMA`:** a question gains an optional `probe`, a short topic name. **`QUIZ_SYSTEM`:** up to 2 of the 10 may be probes: `goal` left empty, pitched at level 2 (one step, wrong choices clearly off), under the same safety rules.
- **`Question`** gains `probe: str = ""`.

### 2. Checking (`check_questions`, `fits`)

- **A probe topic is dropped** (the question stays only if its goal is on the map, like any question) when it:
  - breaks the name rules (`check_name`);
  - is on the map (`m.find`, aliases too);
  - mentions anything set aside;
  - was probed in the last 30 days, or is already held.
- **`fits`:** a probe question fits while its topic is still off the map and nothing it shows is set aside; it has no goal id, so `_still_on` is skipped for it. A topic added to the map, or set aside, after it was written drops it before the race.
- **The blind check** (`_verify`) covers probes like every question.

### 3. In the race (`RaceDesk.start`, `pick`, `_log`)

- **At most one probe per race,** at question 2 or 3 (by the desk's `rng`); question 4 stays the bonus's. Other probes stay held for later races.
- **It counts for the place** like any question, and is read aloud the same way.
- **It never touches levels, review, or goal boxes:** `_remember` leaves it out of the concept ledger, `pick` skips `concepts.answered` and `concepts.practiced`, and `_spaced` already skips a question without a goal id.
- **The results line:** `goal_id: ""`, `goal: ""`, `probe: <topic>`, with `right` and `seconds` as today; never the question's words. `week()` and `asked_since()` already skip lines without a goal.
- **`ResultsLog.probes(username, since)`:** each probed topic since a day, newest first, with whether it was tapped.

### 4. After the race (`race.py`, `conversation.py`)

- **`RaceDesk.probes_after(username, race, today)`:** up to two topics: this race's probe, and the latest other untapped probe from the last 7 days, both still off the map and not set aside.
- **`RaceDesk.tap_probe(username, topic, today)`:** only a topic that person was offered in the last 7 days (a forged one is refused, returning None). It logs `{"day", "probe_tap": topic}` and returns the fixed opener: "Let's talk about {topic}! What do you already know about it?" No Claude call.
- **`Conversations.open_with(username, text)`:** appends the opener as Lerni's turn, in memory only. It's refused while a reply is still coming, and a second identical opener in a row isn't added again. The student's answer is then a normal exchange: the tagger sees the opener as Lerni's last message and adds the topic only from the student's own words.

### 5. The screen (`web/games.py`, `web/main.py`)

- **`games.py`:** at the finish, under the result line, "Want to find out more?" and up to two buttons, "🔭 earthquakes". Hidden on Race again, mid-race, and when there are none.
- **`main.py`** (it owns the tabs): a tap opens Chat with the opener showing: a supervised student's `home`, an educator's `ask`.
- **Who gets probes:** every race, the educator's too (educators race). Independent students who aren't educators don't have Games.

## Rules

- **Rule 5:** a probe never adds or changes a goal. Its topic joins the map only from the student's words in Chat.
- **Rule 6:** the results log keeps labels and numbers only (the topic and the question's hash); the opener lives in the in-memory conversation like any Lerni message.
- **Safety:** the supervised safety rules cover probes. Nothing set aside is probed; a topic naming a set-aside, even inside a longer name, is dropped.

## Review focus

1. **No sources:** a map with no strong interests and no recently explained goals gets no probe lines and no probes.
2. **A held probe goes stale:** the topic is added or set aside after it was written; `fits` drops it before the race.
3. **Two quick taps** on a topic button, or a tap while Chat is answering: the opener is added once, or refused with a friendly line.
4. **The buttons clear** on Race again, and never show mid-race or over a bonus.
5. **A race with both:** a drawn bonus at question 4 and a probe at 2 or 3 never collide, and the question the bonus replaces goes back as today.
