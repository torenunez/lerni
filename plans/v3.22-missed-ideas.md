# v3.22 Missed ideas come back: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An idea the student got wrong comes back in new words after a gap, until they get it right three times in a row. This is spaced review, which Version 4's recall builds on.

**Architecture:**
- **The ledger (`concepts.py`):** each concept gets `due` (an ISO date, or none) and `step` (0, 1 or 2).
  - **A wrong answer:** `step` 0, due tomorrow.
  - **A right answer on a concept with a due date:**
    - step 0 → step 1, due in 3 days;
    - step 1 → step 2, due in 7 days;
    - at step 2, it's retired (`due` cleared).
  - **A right answer on a concept with no due date:** changes nothing.
  - **The calls:**
    - `answered(username, concept, right, today=None)` applies the rule;
    - `due(username, today) -> list[tuple[str, str]]` returns `(goal, concept)` pairs due today or earlier, oldest due date first.
  - **Old ledgers** load with no due dates. Labels, dates, and numbers only.
- **The race (`race.py`):**
  - **`_write`** takes up to 4 due concepts, 2 a race, whose goal is still on the map and that mention nothing set aside. It passes them to `quiz_input(…, due=…)`.
  - **The prompt line:** "Due for review (ask each idea again in new words; use its label as the concept): halves of odd numbers (Fractions); …".
  - **`QUIZ_SYSTEM`:** the "never an idea listed as recently asked" rule gets "except ideas due for review".
  - **Repeat rules:**
    - due concepts are taken out of the 14-day concept list that both Claude and `check_questions` see;
    - the near-same-words rule still applies, so a review is asked in new words.
  - **Matching a review to its idea:** Claude may write the label a little differently ("half of odd numbers"). After the checks, a question whose concept is close to a due label (a `difflib` ratio of at least 0.8, after `normalize`) takes the due label exactly. So its answer moves that idea's schedule.
  - **Skipped reviews:** if Claude skips a due concept, it stays due. Nothing is lost for being overdue.
- **The Practice line (`results.py`):** missed ideas are matched by `normalize`, so "Thirds" and "thirds" are one (deferred from v3.17). The words stay "missed lately".

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+ standard library (`difflib`), pytest; `scripts/eval_race.py` (real Claude calls, run by hand).

**Spec:** [spec 09b](specs/09b-harder-races.md), "Review". The admin's decisions of 2026-10-19 (the idea comes back as a label with a due date, asked fresh; up to 2 a race; 1, 3, 7 days).

## Global Constraints

- **Rule 6:** the ledger holds labels, goal names, dates, and counts; never question text.
- **Rule 5:** reviews never touch the map.
- **A race is never refused because of reviews:** with no due concepts, or none written, the race is as today.
- **Tests:**
  1. `test_race.py`, `test_a_missed_idea_comes_back_on_schedule`:
     - a wrong answer makes the idea due tomorrow;
     - tomorrow, the prompt lists it, and a question on it isn't refused as a repeat;
     - a near label ("half of odd numbers") counts as that idea;
     - right answers move it to 3 days, then 7, then retire it.
  2. The existing results test gains "Thirds" and "thirds" counting as one missed idea.
- **Eval:** one case in `eval_race.py`: a due concept is asked again, in different words from the question it came from. Run by hand once.

## Review Focus

1. **A due idea whose goal was removed or renamed** (labels are goal names): it's skipped while its goal isn't on the map, and returns if the goal does.
2. **Two near labels both due:** a question matches the closest one only.
3. **A due idea that was also asked in the last 14 days:** it's exempt, but only from the concept rule, never from the words rule.
4. **Held questions written before an idea became due:** they race as they are; the review comes in the next set.
5. **The educator's own races:** their own ledger.
