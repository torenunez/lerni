# v3.21 Next after a wrong answer: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** After a wrong answer, the student sees the question they just answered with the right answer and its why, then taps **Next**. Today the why shows under the next question, so it's easy to miss and mixes two questions.

**Architecture (`web/games.py` only; no change to `race.py`):**
- **A pure helper,** `question_card(race, last=None) -> tuple[str, bool]`, gives the card's Markdown, and whether the choices show:
  - **after a wrong answer, mid-race** (`last` is that answer's `(number, Turn)` and `not turn.right`):
    - `Question 2 of 5`;
    - **the question just answered**;
    - `Not this time: it's **4**. Half means 2 equal parts.`

    The choices are hidden.
  - **otherwise:** today's card, the current question with its choices.
- **The Next button:**
  - `next_btn = gr.Button("Next", variant="primary", elem_classes="lerni-go", visible=False)`, under the card, shown only with a waiting card;
  - tapping it redraws the current race (`desk.current(user)`) with no line, which shows the next question.
- **The answer handler:** `on_choice` passes the answer to `screen(…, last=…)`, and the note line no longer repeats the why for a wrong answer mid-race.
- **What doesn't change:**
  - right answers move straight on, as today ("Right answer: boost! …");
  - the last answer shows the finish, as today, with its why if it was wrong;
  - the track and the sounds update at the tap, as today.
- **A refresh, or opening Games again,** shows the current question (the waiting card lives only on screen).

**Tech Stack:** Gradio 6.30 (existing components, no `gr.render`), pytest.

**Spec:** the to-do row (the admin's reordering of 2026-10-22). The student PRD's race story ("show the right answer with a short why") is unchanged.

## Global Constraints

- **The page never holds a future answer:** the waiting card shows only the question already answered and its right answer, which the server has already revealed.
- **Phone first (rule 9):**
  - Next is at least 48 px tall;
  - checked at phone size on a throwaway localhost server, with a generated test account deleted afterwards (a few small Claude calls to write the questions).
- **Tests:** one new test, `tests/student/test_games.py`, `test_a_wrong_answer_waits_for_next`, covering `question_card`:
  - a wrong answer mid-race shows that question, the right answer, and its why, with no choices;
  - a right answer, or no answer, shows the current question with its choices.

## Review Focus

1. **A double tap on Next,** or Next tapped after the race was dropped (another tab): it shows the current state, never an error.
2. **A wrong last answer:** the finish shows, with no Next.
3. **A screen reader:** after a wrong answer, the card is read (the note is already a live region; the card's text changes).
4. **The sputter sound and the track** still fire at the tap, not at Next.
5. **Choices hidden while waiting:** a stale tap on an old choice button can't answer the next question (the `shown` ref still guards it).
