# v3.45: fewer goal suggestions

**Goal:** The educator sees at most one goal suggestion at a time, for a goal the student has really outgrown.

**Why (2026-11-10):** production's first morning plan made 14 goal suggestions, so the educator's page listed 19 items when the admin asked for up to 5.
- "Running out" meant 12 or more different ideas in 30 days.
- At the student's pace, most goals pass that within two weeks, however broad they are.
- Of the student's goals, only a third are at the top level, so that's a truer sign that a goal could widen.

## What changes (`morning.py`)

- **When to widen a goal:** only at the top level (5), with 12 or more different ideas in 30 days.
  - **Ranked by:** first the ideas it asked again after the student had them right, which means real repetition and not reviews of misses; then by how many ideas.
  - **Only one at a time:** the call marks only the first-ranked goal "running out", and only while no goal suggestion is waiting.
  - **The existing pauses stay:** a goal whose suggestion was used or dismissed waits 14 days.
- **Steps don't change:** goals with 12 or more ideas still get steps. They're internal, never shown, and they keep races varied.
- **Tidying:** each morning keeps at most one waiting goal suggestion, the first-ranked, and drops the rest. So production's extra 13 go at the first morning call after the deploy, which runs at start-up.
- **The log line is unchanged.**

No screen changes, so there's no mockup.

## Deploying

Safe on the home server while in use: pull, reinstall, restart. A restart clears open conversations. The morning call at start-up tidies the extra goal suggestions within a couple of minutes. Nothing to set up.
