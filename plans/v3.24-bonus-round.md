# v3.24 The bonus round: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Now and then, question 4 asks the student to explain an idea in their own words, typed or spoken, and Claude grades it against its key ideas. It's a way back from last place, and it fits goals multiple choice can't reach (feelings, philosophy, "explain how").

**Started before the 2026-11-03 check:** the admin's call on 2026-10-23. The bonus round doesn't change difficulty levels (bonus answers never count toward them), so the check can still read the multiple-choice results.

**Architecture:**
- **Writing (`race.py`):**
  - `QUIZ_SCHEMA` gains an optional `bonus` list: `goal`, `concept`, `question` ("Explain how …" / "Why …"), and `key_ideas`, 2 or 3 short phrases.
  - `QUIZ_SYSTEM` asks for 2 per set: an idea from a goal, in their interests, answerable in a few sentences. The same safety rules apply (never scary or sad, nothing set aside).
  - **`check_bonus`** drops one that:
    - has a question over 140 characters;
    - has fewer than 2 or more than 3 key ideas, or a key idea over 60 characters;
    - names a goal not on the map;
    - mentions anything set aside;
    - repeats a recent concept (due ideas are exempt, as in v3.22).
  - **Held:** bonuses are kept in memory beside the day's questions (`_bonuses`), and a race takes one at its start.
- **When (`race.py`):**
  - **The race:** `Race` gets `bonus: Bonus | None`, `lucky: bool` (a 1-in-4 draw at the start, from an injectable `rng`), and `bonus_live: bool`.
  - **The decision:** right after the third answer, if the race holds a bonus and (the student is 3rd, or `lucky`), `bonus_live` turns on. Question 4 is then the bonus.
  - **Never more than one** a race.
  - **The question it replaces** goes back into the held set.
  - **The page** sees only the bonus's question, never its key ideas.
- **Grading (`race.py`, `RaceDesk.answer_bonus`):** one small-model call through the existing adapter (`write(system, prompt, schema, timeout)`), at most 20 seconds.
  - **In:** the question, the key ideas, the answer, and which voice they have.
  - **Out:** `tier` (`nailed`, `partly`, `tried`, or `none`), `line` (one kind sentence, at most 120 characters), and `care` (true if the answer says they're hurt or unsafe).
  - **No call** for a blank answer, Skip, or emojis only: that's `none` at once.
  - **If the call fails or times out,** the answer counts as `tried`: "Lerni couldn't read that one. ⭐ +2 for trying."
  - **With `care`,** the card shows Chat's caring words (the same rule as `conversation.py`) instead of feedback, and the answer counts as `tried`.
- **Effects:**
  - **Movement:**
    - nailed and partly move the car like a right answer, and count as right for the place;
    - nailed also slows both rivals once more;
    - tried and none take the small step.
  - **Points:** 15 / 7 / 2 / 0 ⭐, as a separate award with id `<race>-bonus`, so it's applied once.
  - **The map:**
    - **nailed:** the goal is marked explained today (journaled `by="race bonus"`), as a good explanation in Chat does. This is an observation, not a goal change (rule 5).
  - **The review schedule:** partly counts as a miss on the concept (v3.22); nailed counts as right.
  - **Levels:** never moved by bonus answers.
  - **The results log:** a line with `bonus: <tier>`, the goal, the concept, and the question's hash.
  - **The answer:** stays in memory only until it's graded, then it's dropped (rule 6).
- **The screen (`web/games.py`, `web/talk.js`, `web/main.py`):**
  - **The card:** "⭐ Bonus: explain it in your own words!", then the question; a 16 px text box with **Send** and **Skip**; and **Hold to talk** when voice is on.
  - **Hold to talk:** the dock and hidden fields use a third suffix, `bonus`, so `talk.js` drives it as it drives Chat. What's heard is sent at once, as in Chat.
  - **After Send:** the grade line and the ⭐, then **Next**.
  - **Wiring:** `games_tab` gets `speech` from `main.py`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+, Gradio 6.30, vanilla JS, pytest; a new `scripts/eval_bonus.py` (real Claude calls, run by hand).

**Spec:** [spec 09b](specs/09b-harder-races.md), "The bonus round". The admin's decisions of 2026-10-19 (three tiers; offered when behind, and sometimes at random) and of 2026-10-20 (it moves the car).

## Global Constraints

- **Rule 5:** the bonus never adds a goal or changes one; "explained" is the same observation Chat's tagger records.
- **Rule 6:** the answer text is never written to disk or printed. The results log gets the tier and labels; there are no conversation logs for races.
- **Supervised safety:** both the writing and the grading prompts carry the rules. `care` comes first.
- **The page never holds key ideas or a grade before the server gives one.**
- **Tests:**
  1. `test_race.py`, `test_the_bonus_comes_when_behind_or_lucky_and_counts_once`:
     - last after question 3 gets the bonus, and so does a lucky draw;
     - never two;
     - the public view has no key ideas;
     - each tier moves the car as specified;
     - the award applies once.
  2. `test_race.py`, `test_a_bonus_answer_is_graded_safely`:
     - blank or emojis is `none` with no call;
     - a grader failure is `tried`;
     - `care` shows the caring words;
     - the answer is in no file under the data folder.
- **Evals:**
  - `eval_bonus.py`, 3 cases: a full answer is nailed; half of the key ideas is partly; emojis are none;
  - `eval_race.py`: a set comes with 1 or 2 bonuses, each with 2 or 3 key ideas.

  Run by hand once.
- **Screen check:** at phone size, on a throwaway localhost server with a generated test account deleted afterwards: a typed bonus to the finish, and Skip. Hold to talk is checked by the admin on the iPad.

## Review Focus

1. **A double tap on Send,** a Send after the race was dropped, and a Send while grading is still running: graded once, awarded once.
2. **A spoken answer Whisper misheard:** it's graded as heard. The card shows what was heard, so the student sees why.
3. **The 1-in-4 draw and "3rd after question 3":** it's never offered at question 5, and never when no bonus was written.
4. **The grader's line and the safety rules:** nothing scary or sad; and `care` only for real worry, not for "my brakes are broken" in a car answer.
5. **Points pacing:** a nailed bonus is worth 15 ⭐ on top of the race. Check that "about N races" on the garage still reads sensibly.
