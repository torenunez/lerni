# v4.10: typed answers and Spelling

**Goal:** the student hears a word, types it, and learns to spell it: the class list first, then words Lerni picks from their interests. The first answer Lerni grades by code from typed text ([spec 11](specs/11-new-ways-to-answer.md#spelling)).

## The admin's calls (2026-11-17)

- **Two sources of words:** the class list the educator types or pastes, and Lerni's own picks, which go straight into practice. The educator can remove any word.
- **The class list comes first.** Lerni's picks fill in once it runs low or is learned.
- **A round is 5 words:** 2 ⭐ for each one spelled right, plus 5 for all 5 (up to 15; a race pays up to 20). Spelling stops paying after 50 ⭐ a day; practice goes on.
- **A photo of the list comes later,** in its own small row: Upload can't read images yet.
- **Typed only:** autocorrect, suggestions, and capitals are off in the answer field.

## My calls

- **Its own ☰ section, "Spelling",** next to Games, for a supervised student and for educators (who try it). Its points go into the same total as races, so they still buy cars.
- **A spelling level, 1 to 6,** like race levels: it starts at 1, goes up after a round with 4 or 5 right, and down after 2 or fewer. It steers only Lerni's picks: 1 is short words with short vowels; 6 is longer words with prefixes and suffixes.
- **Lerni's picks lean on adjectives and on words with prefixes and suffixes** ("unhappy", "careful"), drawn from the student's general interests. After a right answer, a word with a prefix or suffix shows its parts (un · happy). Tapping where a word splits waits for later.
- **Missed words come back the next day,** and each right answer pushes a word further out: 1, 3, 7, then 14 days. After 4 right in a row it counts as learned and leaves the rounds.
- **A new class list replaces the old one;** earlier words stay in practice as Lerni's until learned, so their counts are kept. Removed words are remembered, so Lerni never picks them again.
- **Without the voice** (speech off), the Spelling section stays hidden, since every word is heard.

## How a round goes

1. **Tap Start:** Lerni picks 5 words.
   - **First:** class words that are due, oldest due first.
   - **Then:** class words never asked, Lerni's picks never asked, and other due words.
   - **If fewer than 10 unasked picks are left,** one small-model call writes 10 more after the round starts.
2. **Each word is spoken:** "fossil. The fossil was millions of years old. fossil." 🔊 replays it.
3. **He types the word and taps Check, or presses Return.** Code grades it: ignoring case and outer spaces, with curly apostrophes made straight.
4. **Right:** "✓ fossil, +2 ⭐". **Missed:** "It's fossil", with the letters he got wrong highlighted. His attempt shows only on screen and is never saved.
5. **After the fifth word:** the round's points, and Start again.

## Code

- **`spelling.py`:**
  - **`SpellingStore`:** `spelling/<user>.json` holds the level, then each word: its word, source (class or lerni), sentence, box, due date, right and missed counts, a streak, and mistake-label counts. Also the removed words. One lock per file, written atomically, and a broken file is moved aside, as in `concepts.py`.
  - **`mistake(word, typed) -> str`:** one label for a missed word: `double` (a doubled letter missing or added), `ie-ei`, `silent-e`, `swap` (two letters swapped), `missing`, `extra`, `vowel`, or `other`.
  - **`SpellingDesk`:** picks a round, grades an answer, awards points (`spell-<round id>`), and moves the level. Its Claude calls run through the `Quizzer`:
    - Lerni's picks, as JSON `{words: [{word, sentence, kind}]}`;
    - sentences for new class words.
  - **Checks on Claude's words:** lowercase letters only, 3–14 letters; the sentence uses the word whole, in at most 120 characters; nothing already kept or removed. The interests go into the prompt fenced as data.
- **`points.py`:** `award` takes `source="spelling"`, with a daily cap of 50.
- **`web/spelling.py`:**
  - **`spelling_tab`, the student's screen:** Start, the word card with 🔊, an answer field (autocorrect off, set by JS), Check, feedback, and the round's total.
  - **The educator's fold** on the student's page: the class list (a text box and Save list), the words with checkboxes to remove, and "missed most" with mistake labels.
- **`web/main.py`:** the `spelling` tab for supervised students and educators. **`web/app.py`:** the store and desk, sharing the race's `Quizzer` and the voice.
- **Rule 6** (CLAUDE.md): add "spelling words (chosen by the educator or Lerni), their sentences, and counts and mistake labels; never what the student typed".

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **The happy path:** a class list plus a fake's picks gives a round of 5. Right and missed answers are graded, points are awarded, and a missed word is due tomorrow while a right one moves out.
- **`mistake`:** one case per label.
- **The guarantees:**
  - a typed attempt is never written to disk (rule 6);
  - Claude's words that fail the checks are dropped;
  - a removed word never comes back.

## Eval (real calls, run by hand)

- **`scripts/eval_spelling.py`:** Lerni's picks at levels 1 and 5 for a made-up map (dinosaurs, LEGO), and sentences for a short class list. It prints them to read by eye and asserts that every one passes the checks. That's about 3 small calls.
