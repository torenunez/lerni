## My calls

- **One schedule** (`schedule.py`, new): every job time in one place, in the home server's local time.

  | Job | When |
  |---|---|
  | The night job | 02:00 |
  | Its retries | every 30 minutes until 06:00 |
  | The copy and stats rebuild | 03:00 (the LaunchAgent) |

  - `race.py` and `backup.py` read their times from it.
  - The admin reference gets one table.
  - Changing a time means one edit and a restart; the copy also needs `lerni backup install` again.
- **The night job,** one run per supervised student, in order:
  1. **Yesterday's held questions, passages and prompts go** (rule 6), as at 05:00 now.
  2. **Bridges shortlisted by meaning:**
     - the local embedding model embeds each top-level interest and each goal (its name and notes);
     - code ranks every interest–goal pair not yet bridged, waiting, in use, dismissed, or set aside, closest first;
     - the top `SHORTLIST` (12, provisional) go to the morning call.
     
     Without the embedder, the morning plan runs as now, unranked, and the log says so.
  3. **The morning plan proposes connections only from the shortlist**, checked in code. It's otherwise unchanged; waiting connections still need the educator's tick.
  4. **The day's supply:**
     - races' worth for the normal races left today: 10 a day, less 3 reading races and 1 writing race, so 6, not the row's 12, which came before reading and writing races;
     - plus the day's first passage, so the 2nd race doesn't wait on it.
     
     Ticked connections steer the questions as now. During the day, prefill writes only when the supply runs out (`ready`), as now.
- **Log for analysis** (Rules of ML #29: keep what the system saw when it decided):
  - **On each connection the morning plan proposes:**
    - its `similarity` and `rank` in the shortlist;
    - the shortlist's size that night.
    
    These are numbers, in `suggestions/<user>.json` beside its state.
  - **A tick, a dismissal, and a lapse** already change its state, with the day; that's the label.
  - **The stats copy gains a `connections` table** (rank, similarity, state, days waiting), so the tick rate by rank is one query.
- **Why only the shortlist:** if the model could still propose any pair, the rank would mean nothing.
- **A risk to watch:** names close in meaning may be the obvious pairs, not the surprising ones a good hook needs. The shortlist is generous (12 for at most 5 waiting), and the tick rate by rank shows which ranks get ticked; the cut-off follows that, in v4.18.
- **No screen changes,** so no mockup. The educator sees connections exactly as now.

## Rule 6
