# v3.37: areas set automatically

**Goal:** Every goal has a subject area without anyone sorting it, and a person moves one with a tap.

**Why (the admin, 2026-11-03):** 23 of the student's 24 goals sat under "Other" because they predate areas (v3.28), and sorting them took a Suggest-box request and a tick each. The admin doesn't want to curate areas: Lerni sets them, a person can undo or reassign.

**The decision:** this reverses v3.28's "an area is set only by a person's tap". Areas only organize the list and the progress card; goals and their notes stay rule 5 (a person's tap).

## What changes

- **The sorter (`src/lerni/student/area_sorter.py`, new):**
  - `AreaSorter(writer, maps)` with `sort(username) -> int` (goals given an area).
  - It takes the goals with no area, asks Claude once (the small model, through the race's `Quizzer.write` with its own schema, 20 s), and saves the answers in one `maps.change(..., by="lerni")`, so each sort is one journal line `lerni map undo` reverses.
  - The prompt sees the map's goals already in areas (how this family sorts) and `areas(m)` (the map's areas, then the starters). It picks one of those, or a new short one only when none fits, the same new one for goals that belong together.
  - Only goals still without an area when the answer comes back are changed; a name that isn't one of the asked goals, or an area that isn't a plain name, is dropped.
  - A goal Claude couldn't sort isn't asked again until the server restarts (no loop of calls); a failure never fails the save that triggered it.
- **When it runs:** `MapStore` gains an `after_change(username)` hook, called after a save once the lock is released; the app sets it to the sorter. Every way a goal arrives (the list's Add, Upload, the Suggest box, Put back) is covered by one wiring point. The sorter's own save and saves with no unsorted goal make no call. At start, one background sweep sorts any map with goals and no area.
- **The picker (`web/mentallist.py`):** on an open goal, the read-only "Area: Math" fact becomes a row: an **Area** dropdown (the map's areas and the starters, a new one can be typed) and a **Move** button. A move is a person's change (`by="person:<username>"`), shown at once under its new heading. Shown wherever Rename is.
- **The Suggest box** keeps its area proposals ("move Fractions to Music" still works); new goals it proposes keep their area.

## Tests (minimal, fakes only)

- `tests/student/test_area_sorter.py`, the happy path: a fake writer sorts two goals (one into a map area, one into a new area); a goal a person already put in an area is untouched; the journal line says `lerni`; a second `sort` makes no call.
- One safety test: a writer that raises leaves the save done, the goal in Other, and isn't called again for that goal.
- The picker: one test that moving a goal through the list's handler sets its area as a person's change.
- `scripts/eval_areas.py`: 3 cases with real Claude (school subjects into starters; two related goals into one new area; an existing family area reused).

## Deploying

Safe to put on the home server while in use: pull, reinstall, restart (a restart clears open conversations). At start the sweep sorts any goals with no area on every map, as one journaled change per map; `lerni map log <user>` shows it; a goal moved to Other stays there. One small-model call per sort.
