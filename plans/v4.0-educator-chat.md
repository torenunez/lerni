# v4.0: Version 4, the educator's Chat (the plan)

**Goal:** The educator is a **continuous improvement partner and auditor, not a gatekeeper** (the admin, 2026-11-10).
- They check a small random sample of race questions.
- They give broad feedback in their own words, which improves every question after it.
- They can hand Lerni an answer key.
- Races mix in the questions they've checked or written. A question they mark wrong never comes back.

**Why:**
- Claude writes every race question, and the blind second check catches most wrong answer keys, but nothing learns from the educator yet.
- The Tell Lerni box (v3.44) is already an educator chat that proposes changes to tick, so Version 4 builds on it instead of adding a screen.

## The admin's decisions (2026-11-10)

- **Sample, don't approve:**
  - the educator sees up to 3 questions a day, drawn at random from recent races, never the whole list;
  - nothing waits on their approval;
  - races go on as now.
- **Feedback first, in one box:**
  - Tell Lerni, on top, takes broad freeform feedback ("too easy", "use metric units", "the second one's answer is wrong");
  - its agent proposes a **guidance note**, or marks on the sampled questions, to tick;
  - a ticked guidance note steers every later question set and the morning plan.
- **Quick marks:**
  - tick sampled questions, then **Right ✓** or **Wrong ✗**, as in v3.44's tick lists;
  - no list that redraws itself (`gr.render`).
- **Up to 1 checked question a race,** from that race's goals. Each banked question waits 7 days before it races again.
- **Rule 6:**
  - the sample stays in memory, like held questions today (a restart empties it until the next race);
  - on disk:
    - the **checked bank**: questions an educator marked right or gave as an answer key, which rule 6 already plans for;
    - **wrong marks**: a question's hash, plus the why the educator gave;
    - **guidance notes**: the educator's own words.
  - never a student's words.

## The PRs

- **v4.1, check a few questions:**
  - a **Check a few questions** section on the student's page, under Suggestions from Lerni:
    - up to 3 of today's raced questions, drawn at random, with Claude's answer and the wrong choices;
    - Right ✓ and Wrong ✗;
    - a line for the week: "6 checked · 5 right · 1 wrong · bank: Fractions 5".
  - **The checked bank (`bank.py`, new):**
    - ✓ saves the question, its choices, its answer, its why, and its goal id;
    - ✗ saves the question's hash, so it never races again.
  - **Races:** up to 1 banked question a race, for a goal in that race's set, not again for 7 days. The blind check and the set-aside checks still apply.
  - **Kept in memory:** raced questions, the last 30 per student, so the sample can be drawn.
- **v4.2, feedback and answer keys through Tell Lerni:**
  - **The agent sees** today's sample and the student's guidance notes, and gains two proposal kinds:
    - **guidance** ("a bit harder; metric units"), kept as the latest 10 notes, read by the question writer and the morning plan;
    - **mark**, a ✗ with a why on a sampled question ("the second one's answer is wrong: ⅝ is left").
  - **Answer keys:** a typed or attached worksheet (.txt, .md, .docx, as today) becomes question-and-answer proposals for a goal. Claude writes two wrong choices for each, and a ticked pair goes into the bank.
  - **Claude sees the whys:** a ✗'s why reaches that goal's question writing ("The educator marked these wrong: …").
  - **Another student:** if the educator names one, the agent says to pick that student first. It never proposes for a student who isn't shown.

Each PR has its plan, tests (fakes only), a fresh review, and a phone check by the admin.
