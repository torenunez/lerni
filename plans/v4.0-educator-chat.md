# v4.0: Version 4, the educator's Chat (the plan)

**Goal:** The educator is a **continuous improvement partner and auditor, not a gatekeeper** (the admin, 2026-11-10).
- They check a small random sample of race questions.
- They give broad feedback in their own words, which improves every question after it.
- They can hand Lerni an answer key.
- Races mix in the questions they've checked or written. A question they mark wrong never comes back.

**Why:**
- Claude writes every race question, and the blind second check catches most wrong answer keys, but nothing learns from the educator yet.
- The Tell Lerni box (v3.44) is already an educator chat that proposes changes to tick, so Version 4 builds on it instead of adding a screen.

## The admin's decisions (2026-11-10)

- **Sample, don't approve:**
  - the educator sees up to 3 questions a day, drawn at random from recent races, never the whole list;
  - the draw is **stratified** (the admin, 2026-11-10):
    - **Across goals:** the 3 questions come from 3 different goals, the ones with the fewest checks in the last 30 days first, so every goal is audited in turn.
    - **Toward misses:** within a goal, a question the student got wrong is twice as likely to be drawn, since a miss may mean a bad question;
    - **Across days** (the admin, 2026-11-10): goals with no feedback yet come first, then the fewest checks in 30 days, then the longest since the last one. Only a mark counts (✓, ✗, or a guidance note naming the goal), never a question just shown, so feedback a little at a time over several days still reaches every goal. Days away don't matter, and after a mark, the next draw that day skips the goals just checked;
  - nothing waits on their approval;
  - races go on as now.
- **Feedback first, in one box:**
  - Tell Lerni, on top, takes broad freeform feedback ("too easy", "use metric units", "the second one's answer is wrong");
  - its agent proposes a **guidance note**, or marks on the sampled questions, to tick;
  - a ticked guidance note steers every later question set and the morning plan.
- **Quick marks:**
  - tick sampled questions, then **Right ✓** or **Wrong ✗**, as in v3.44's tick lists;
  - no list that redraws itself (`gr.render`).
- **Up to 1 checked question a race,** from that race's goals. Each banked question waits 7 days before it races again.
- **Learned questions stop, with no educator needed** (the admin, 2026-11-10):
  - a banked question answered right 3 times in a row retires (a miss resets the count);
  - an idea answered right every time, at least twice, leaves races, kept on its own list rather than "asked lately", but stays open for Version 6 recall (the admin, 2026-11-10, after two outside reviews: a first success isn't lasting memory);
  - missed ideas keep their review schedule (back after 1, 3, then 7 days, retired after three right).
- **Rule 6:**
  - the sample stays in memory, like held questions today (a restart empties it until the next race);
  - on disk:
    - the **checked bank**: questions an educator marked right or gave as an answer key, which rule 6 already plans for;
    - **wrong marks**: a question's hash, plus the why the educator gave;
    - **guidance notes**: the educator's own words.
  - never a student's words.

## The PRs

- **v4.6, check a few questions:**
  - a **Check a few questions** section on the student's page, under Suggestions from Lerni:
    - up to 3 of today's raced questions, stratified (`sample`: different goals, the least checked first, missed questions weighted ×2), with Claude's answer and the wrong choices;
    - Right ✓ and Wrong ✗;
    - a line for the week: "6 checked · 5 right · 1 wrong · bank: Fractions 5".
  - **The checked bank (`bank.py`, new):**
    - ✓ saves the question, its choices, its answer, its why, and its goal id;
    - ✗ saves the question's hash, so it never races again.
  - **Races:** up to 1 banked question a race, for a goal in that race's set, not again for 7 days, and retired after 3 right in a row. The blind check and the set-aside checks still apply.
  - **Learned ideas (`concepts.py`):** an idea right every time, at least twice, leaves races (its own list, read by the question writer as "learned, don't race") and stays open for Version 6 recall.
  - **Kept in memory:** raced questions, the last 30 per student, so the sample can be drawn.
- **v4.7, feedback and answer keys through Tell Lerni:**
  - **The agent sees** today's sample and the student's guidance notes, and gains two proposal kinds:
    - **guidance** ("a bit harder; metric units"), kept as the latest 10 notes, read by the question writer and the morning plan;
    - **mark**, a ✗ with a why on a sampled question ("the second one's answer is wrong: ⅝ is left").
  - **Answer keys:** a typed or attached worksheet (.txt, .md, .docx, as today) becomes question-and-answer proposals for a goal. Claude writes two wrong choices for each, and a ticked pair goes into the bank.
  - **Claude sees the whys:** a ✗'s why reaches that goal's question writing ("The educator marked these wrong: …").
  - **Another student:** if the educator names one, the agent says to pick that student first. It never proposes for a student who isn't shown.

Each PR has its plan, tests (fakes only), a fresh review, and a phone check by the admin.

## Added after two outside reviews (2026-11-10)

- **A ✗ corrects the student too:** it undoes that question's miss in the ledger (a false miss would be due tomorrow) and shows a one-time correction card, in the educator's words, at the next race.
- **A ✗ removes that question everywhere:** the bank, held sets, and any write still running.
- **The sample is drawn from questions actually raced,** not merely written.
- **Fresh questions keep most of a race:** the questions that aren't fresh (bonus, probe, reviews, banked) take at most 3 of 5.
- **Banked questions:** reuse the ideas' review steps (from 7 days), and shuffle the choices so "3 right in a row" isn't memory of one layout. "Retired" means out of races, still open to recall.
- **✓ can be undone the same day;** the answer shows after a tap on the question.
- **Counts, not a rate:** misses are weighted ×2, so "6 checked · 1 wrong" is a work queue, never an error rate.
- **v4.7:** a "harder"/"easier" note becomes a proposed level change to tick, so notes don't fight the level ladder; notes are scoped to a goal or all, and can be revoked; the educator's own keys are blind-checked too (the educator wins a disagreement), and notes warn against names, since they go into every question prompt.
- **Kept on disk (the admin, 2026-11-10; rule 6):** a ✗'d question's record (question, choices, key, the educator's why, goal id) instead of only a hash, so a correction can be explained and a fixed version can return; and the day's sample, deleted after 7 days, so a deploy doesn't empty it.
