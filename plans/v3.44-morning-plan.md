# v3.44: the morning plan

**Goal:** Each morning Lerni looks over each supervised student's map and suggests ways to reach their goals:
- **connections to try** between their interests and goals;
- **goal suggestions** when a goal is running out of things to ask.

The educator ticks what they want, and races and Chat aim for it. Narrow goals also get **goal steps**, so their race questions keep finding something new.

**Why (the admin, 2026-11-10):**
- Questions should "fully leverage the MentalMap and suggest new bridge concepts", and suggestions should be part of the daily batch.
- The admin consolidated three pieces into this one morning call: bridge suggestions, the widen-goal proposal, and the idea lists planned for v3.46.
- A narrow goal in production already gave 15 ideas in 18 questions.

## Words (the admin's choice, 2026-11-10)

- **Connection to try:** one interest, one goal, and the hook that links them, in one line.
  - Example: "trains → Fractions: a train's seats shared out equally".
  - It's a teaching angle, not a question and not a bridge.
- **Goal suggestion:** a proposed change to a goal's notes when it's running out of things to ask.
  - Example: "Fractions: halves and quarters, then thirds".
- **Goal steps:** the small steps inside a narrow goal that races work through.
  - Labels only, never shown, never on the map.
- **Bridge:** unchanged. It's recorded only when a conversation really leads from an interest to a goal.

"Idea" stays the word for a race question's label.

## What changes

- **The morning call (`src/lerni/student/morning.py`, new):**
  - **When:** at 5:00 with the race write-ahead, and at start-up if it hasn't run today. One small-model call for each supervised student, and production only, like the write-ahead.
  - **It reads:**
    - the goals with their notes and levels;
    - the interest groups;
    - existing bridges;
    - each goal's ideas from the last 30 days;
    - the connections already in use, waiting, or dismissed;
    - the educator's recent notes on suggestions.

    Everything is fenced off as information.
  - **It returns, as structured JSON (a fake in tests):**
    - **up to 5 connections to try**: never a pair already bridged, waiting, in use, or dismissed;
    - **a goal suggestion** for a goal running out (12 or more different ideas in 30 days), at most one waiting a goal;
    - **goal steps** (10 to 15 short labels) for a goal running out that has none, or whose steps are all tried, or whose notes changed.
  - **The checks:**
    - every interest and goal must be on the map and not set aside;
    - the hook and each step are short plain labels (under 60 characters);
    - anything that fails a check is dropped.

    If the call fails, the morning has no suggestions, and races and Chat run as today.
- **What's kept (`<data>/suggestions/<username>.json`):**
  - **The items:**
    - connections and goal suggestions, each with its state (waiting, in use, or dismissed) and a date;
    - goal steps by goal id, with the notes they were written for;
    - the educator's last 5 notes about suggestions, as they wrote them.
  - **Rule 6 addition:**
    - these are labels written by Claude plus the educator's feedback, never the student's words;
    - they're added to rule 6's list in CLAUDE.md and the PRD;
    - dismissed items are kept 90 days, so they aren't suggested again;
    - the nightly copies include the file.
- **The educator's page (`web/educator.py`), one box for everything (the admin's choice, 2026-11-10):**
  - **"Suggest or change a goal" becomes "Tell Lerni":**
    - **The line under the heading:** "A new goal, a change to one, or what you think of the suggestions below."
    - **The placeholder:** "New goal, a change, or 'fewer train ideas'…".
  - **The educator agent (`educator_agent.py`):** it reads the waiting suggestions with the map, and gains one proposal kind, `note`.
    - When a message is about the suggestions ("fewer train ideas, more music"), it proposes "Note for suggestions: …", pre-ticked like today's goal proposals.
    - Tapping Add saves the note (the last 5 are kept), and the next morning's call reads it.
    - It still proposes goal changes as before.
  - **A Suggestions from Lerni section** sits under it:
    - **The list:** a tick list, unticked, of the waiting connections ("trains → Fractions: a train's seats shared out equally"), then any goal suggestion, marked *goal*.
    - **"Use ticked":** a ticked connection is **in use**, and a ticked goal suggestion becomes that goal's notes. It's a person's tap, so the map journal records the educator.
    - **"Not these":** the ticked ones are **dismissed**.
    - **No text box of its own.** Ticks clear after a save, and stay after an error.
    - **Hidden** when nothing is waiting, and for a student without Games.
    - **No redrawing screen (`gr.render`):** the same tick list and buttons as Upload.
- **Races (`race.py`):**
  - **Connections in use steer the deal:** each one leads its goal's first slot in each set (and its second, if the goal has more than two), set in that interest.
  - **The prompt:** each goal's line gains `connection to try: trains (a train's seats shared out equally)`.
  - **Goal steps:** they appear on the goal's line as `steps not tried yet: …`, up to 5, with the instruction to use the step's words as the question's idea label. A step is tried once a race has asked it.
- **Chat (`interests.py`'s map block):** a supervised student's map block gains "Connections to try (the educator chose these)", under "Goals to bridge toward". Chat may use one as an opening, one at a time, never forced.
- **When a connection ends:**
  - once the bridge is recorded on the map, it's done and leaves the list;
  - after 30 days unused, it lapses;
  - if its interest is set aside, it's dropped.
- **Log:** one line per student each morning, numbers only: `Morning plan: 5 connections, 1 goal suggestion, 1 goal's steps`.

## Rules

- **Rule 5:**
  - nothing reaches the map without a person's tap: a ticked goal suggestion is the educator's own change;
  - a connection never becomes a bridge by itself;
  - the morning call only proposes.
- **Rule 6:**
  - labels and the educator's short notes, never the student's words;
  - no sample questions, since that would be question text on disk.

## Tests (minimal, fakes only)

- **`tests/student/test_morning.py`, the happy path:** with a fake planner:
  - the morning saves 5 connections, a goal suggestion and steps as labels;
  - "Use ticked" puts a connection in use, and it leads its goal's slot in the next race plan and reaches Chat's map block;
  - the ticked goal suggestion changes the goal's notes;
  - "Not these" dismisses an item;
  - a note added from the agent's proposal reaches the next morning's call.
- **Safety:**
  - a suggestion naming a set-aside or off-map item is dropped;
  - the morning call never changes the map.
- **Steps:** a narrow goal's untried steps reach the race prompt, and a step asked once leaves the list.

**The eval (real calls, by hand), `scripts/eval_morning.py` (new, 3 cases):**
- connections use real map items, and none pairs an interest already bridged to that goal;
- nothing names a set-aside;
- a narrow goal's steps are distinct and fit its notes.

**By hand:**
- the educator's page at phone size, with the keyboard up;
- one real morning call on a throwaway server.

## Deploying

- **Safe on the home server while in use:** pull, reinstall, restart. The restart clears open conversations.
- **What happens next:**
  - The first morning call runs at start-up. Suggestions appear on the educator's page within a couple of minutes.
  - A new `suggestions/` folder is made in the data folder; nothing else to set up.
- **Rollback:** delete that folder, and it's as if suggestions never ran.
