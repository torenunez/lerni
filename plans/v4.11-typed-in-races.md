# v4.11: typed questions in races (spelling and blocks)

**Goal:** one game. A race switches between multiple choice, spelling, and picture math by itself, giving more of whatever needs reinforcing ([spec 11](specs/11-new-ways-to-answer.md#picture-math)). Money comes in v4.11.1 and rulers in v4.11.2.

## The admin's calls (2026-11-17)

- **Spelling and math are treated the same:** both live inside races, not in sections of their own. The student's Spelling section goes away; the educator's Spelling fold stays.
- **Every race has at least one question of each kind** that's available. The other slots go to whatever needs the most reinforcement, and within a kind, to the content that needs it most.
- **Three PRs:** v4.11 (typed questions in races, spelling moved in, blocks), v4.11.1 (money), v4.11.2 (rulers).
- **Pictures are drawn by code,** with an interest's name as the label ("Dinosaurs: 3 rows of 4"). The blocks are an emoji from a short built-in list matched to the interest (dinosaurs 🦕, LEGO 🧱, race cars 🏎️), or squares when nothing matches. No Claude calls.
- **The race lanes and cars show on every question,** typed ones included.

## My calls

- **Kinds:**
  - **choice:** a race question as today;
  - **spell:** Lerni says a word slowly; the answer is typed only, with autocorrect off;
  - **picture:** code draws blocks; the answer is a typed number on the number keyboard.
- **When a kind is available:**
  - **choice:** always (the race still needs its 5 written questions, as today; a typed question's place goes back unasked, like the bonus's).
  - **spell:** the voice is on and a word is ready (Lerni writes picks when few are left, as now).
  - **picture:** the player has a goal in the Math area.
- **The mix, per race (pure code, `mix` in `race.py`):**
  1. One slot for each available kind.
  2. Each other slot goes to the kind with the highest **need**, which is then halved, so a second slot goes elsewhere unless one kind needs much more. Ties go to choice.
  3. **Need** = the kind's miss rate over its last 10 answers (0 to 1; 0.5 with no history yet) + 0.1 for each item due (up to 0.5).
     - **Due:** missed race ideas due again; spelling words due or never asked; for pictures, 3 until the top level, then 1.
  4. **Bonus races:** question 4 (the bonus spot) stays a choice slot, and one more choice is asked besides it.
  5. **Order:** kinds spread out, so two typed questions never come back to back when it can be helped.
- **Within a kind, the neediest content first:**
  - **spell:** class words due, then new class words, then Lerni's due and new picks (today's order);
  - **picture:** the track's current level, sizes drawn at random within it;
  - **choice:** unchanged (missed ideas come back, as now).
- **Cars and points:** a typed answer moves the cars like a choice: right is a full step and slows the rivals. The race pays as today (place, plus one per right answer). Spelling's own stars, perfect-round bonus, and 50 ⭐ cap go away.
- **Spelling's level** moves after every 5 spelling answers (up at 4 or 5 right, down at 2 or fewer), not after a round.
- **Blocks levels** (one track, moving the same way, every 5 answers):
  1. count all the squares (up to 12);
  2. "3 rows of 4. How many in all?";
  3. the same grid turned sideways: how many now?;
  4. "3 × 4 = ?", with the picture;
  5. the missing piece: "12 squares in 3 rows. How many in each row?" (the rows drawn empty);
  6. as 4 and 5, up to 6 × 6.
- **What's shown after an answer:** right: "✓" and, for a word, its parts; missed: the right answer (for a word, the letters missed marked, as now). A typed answer is never saved.

## Code

- **`race.py`:**
  - `Typed` (frozen): kind, prompt, picture data (`{rows, cols, ask}`), the word for a spelling question, and a key the source checks with. The answer never leaves the server.
  - `Race.typed: dict[int, Typed]`, its slots; `answer()` refuses a typed slot; `answer_typed(race, number, right)` moves the cars (shared with `answer` through one step helper).
  - `mix(needs, lucky, rng) -> list[str]`, the five kinds in order.
  - `RaceDesk`: `start` asks each source for its need and items, then builds the race; `typed_answer(username, race_id, number, text)` has the source grade it, moves the cars, logs it, and pays at the finish as today; `read_aloud` reads a spelling word through the source.
  - **Results log rows** gain `kind` (`spell` or `picture`) with a label (the word, or the track and level); never what was typed. The miss rate per kind comes from the last rows of each kind.
- **A `TypedSource` protocol** (in `race.py`): `need(username, today) -> float | None` (None: not available), `item(username, today) -> Typed | None`, `check(username, item, text, today) -> Checked` (right, the answer to show, the mistake label, the word's parts).
- **`spelling.py`:** `SpellingDesk` becomes a source. Rounds, `_award`, and the per-round level go; the store, picks, sentences, class list, removal, and summary stay. `read_word` takes the word, not a round.
- **`blocks.py`** (new): `BlocksDesk`, a source, and `EMOJI`, the interest-to-emoji list (matched on the map's names and other names). `blocks/<user>.json` holds the level and the last 5 results (numbers only). Questions come from code: sizes drawn within the level, the answer worked out by code.
- **`points.py`:** the Spelling source and its cap go.
- **`web/games.py`:** a typed dock beside the bonus's: the question (with the drawing, an inline SVG drawn from the picture data), an answer field, and Check. Spelling plays its word when shown, and 🔊 replays it. `web/race.js` sets the keyboard attributes on the new field (`inputmode="numeric"` for pictures).
- **`web/spelling.py`:** only the educator's fold stays. **`web/main.py`:** the Spelling section and its EARN line go.
- **`web/app.py`:** the race desk gets the sources (spelling only with the voice).
- **Rule 6** (CLAUDE.md): add "blocks levels and results (numbers only)".

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **Happy path:** a race with a math goal and a due word has one of each kind; a right typed answer moves the car and pays at the finish; a missed word is due tomorrow.
- **`mix`:** the neediest kind gets the spare slots; a kind not available gets none; the bonus spot stays a choice.
- **Blocks:** each level's answer matches its drawing.
- **The guarantees:** nothing typed reaches disk; a typed answer to a choice slot (or the reverse) changes nothing.
