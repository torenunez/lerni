# v4.6.4: harder questions at the top

**Goal:** race questions get hard enough at the top that the student answers about 60–65% right there, so races are won about 1 time in 3 again.

## Why (the win-rate check-in, 2026-11-16)

- **The top levels are too easy.** Levels 4 and 5 are answered right far above the 60% target; levels 1–3 sit near it.
- **Level 5 is a ceiling.** More answers come at level 5 than at any other level, so goals pile up there with nowhere harder to go.
- **The rivals' pace is right.** A win needs 4 of 5 right, which at 60% right is about 1 race in 3. The win rate is high because the questions are easy, not because the rivals are slow.
- **The level steps are right.** Up at 4 of 5 right, down at 2 or fewer; levels 1–3 show they settle near the target.

## The change

- **A level 6** (`MAX_LEVEL` 5 → 6 in `concepts.py`). A goal at 5 that gets 4 of 5 right moves up to 6, as between the other levels.
- **The writer's level 6** (`QUIZ_SYSTEM` in `race.py`), after level 5: "6, three steps or the wrong step in a rival's worked answer, wrong choices near misses only a careful thinker catches." It leans on a question type that is already hard (spot the rival's mistake), whose choices may name the mistake; a choice is otherwise only an answer, never a reason. The same rules still hold: words only, short, no trick questions.
- **Unchanged:** levels 1–5, `UP_AT`, `DOWN_AT`, `START_LEVEL`, and the rivals.
- **A side effect, wanted:** the morning plan suggests widening a goal once it's at the top level (`outgrown` in `morning.py`). That now waits until level 6.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **The happy path:** a goal at 5 that gets 4 of 5 right reaches 6, and the writer's prompt says "level 6".
- **Existing tests** that took 5 as the top use `MAX_LEVEL` instead (the hand-edit clamp, the outgrown suggestion).

## Eval (real calls, run by hand)

- `eval_race.py`'s level case writes the same goal at 1, 5, and 6 and prints them to compare by eye; "level 6 still makes a race" is checked like level 5. About 3 writes and their blind checks.

## Checks after it ships

- **The check-in again, about two weeks later:** right answers by level and races won. The target is about 60–65% right at level 6 and about 1 race in 3 won.
- **If level 6 is too hard** (goals bounce between 5 and 6 below 50% right), soften its wording; if still too easy, sharpen levels 4 and 5 next.
