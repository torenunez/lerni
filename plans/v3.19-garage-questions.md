# v3.19 Race questions from the garage: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** About one race question in five compares the real specs of cars the student owns or can afford, as a bridge from a goal to science and engineering. For example:
- "The P1 does 0–60 in 2.7 s and the M4 in 3.4 s. How much sooner does the P1 get there?";
- "Which pushes harder off the line, all-wheel or rear-wheel drive, and why?".

The numbers come from the admin's garage file, so the server can check the sums.

**Architecture:**
- **Which cars (`garage.py`):**
  - `Garage.in_reach(owned, balance) -> list[Car]` returns the real cars the student owns or can afford (price ≤ balance), the same rule that shows a Showroom card's facts. They're sorted by speed, fastest first, at most 4.
  - Cartoon cars have no specs, so they're never in the list.
  - A car still out of reach is never given to Claude (the admin's decision of 2026-10-19: facts are earned by saving up).
- **The prompt (`race.py`):**
  - **The input:** `quiz_input(…, cars=())` adds a line:
    - `Their cars (exact facts; use only these): McLaren P1: 0–60 mph in 2.7 s, 903 hp, 3.8L twin-turbo V8 hybrid, rear-wheel drive, $1,150,000; BMW M4 …`;
    - with no cars in reach, there's no line.
  - **`QUIZ_SYSTEM`** gets one rule: "When their cars are listed, about 1 question in 5 compares their facts to practice a goal through how cars work (speeding up, power, energy, grip, ratios, big numbers). Use only the listed facts, and put any arithmetic in `math`."
  - **What doesn't change:** the question still practices a goal, and the car is the setting, as an interest is.
- **Wiring:**
  - `RaceDesk(…, garage=None)` keeps the garage. `_write` passes `garage.in_reach(p.cars, p.balance)` from `self.points.get(username)`, at write time, including written-ahead sets.
  - `web/app.py` passes its `Garage`.
  - Tests and an empty garage pass nothing, so there's no line.
- **Checks:**
  - **The answer:** the existing `math` check (v3.14) verifies a worked-out number, and the blind check covers the rest.
  - **The quoted facts:** a question quotes facts that are exact in the prompt. A misquoted spec is caught only by the eval (Review Focus 2).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+ standard library, pytest; `scripts/eval_race.py` (real Claude calls, run by hand).

**Spec:** [spec 09](specs/09-play-and-points.md), "Questions" and "The garage". The admin's ideas and decisions:
- 2026-10-20: compare the garage cars' specs as bridges to science and engineering, as soon as possible;
- 2026-10-19: car facts stay hidden until a car is in reach.

## Global Constraints

- **Rule 6:** the garage file holds car facts, not student text. Questions still live in memory only.
- **Hidden until in reach:** no out-of-reach car's facts reach Claude. That's the safety guarantee this version's test pins.
- **Supervised safety rules** apply as before: car questions are about how cars work, never crashes or danger.
- **Tests:** one new test in `test_garage.py`, `test_only_cars_in_reach_reach_the_race`:
  - `in_reach` lists the owned car and the affordable one, never the locked one;
  - `quiz_input` with them shows their facts and never the locked car's name;
  - with nothing in reach, there's no cars line.
- **Eval:** one case in `eval_race.py`, with two cars in reach and one locked. Pass means:
  - at least one question names one of the two cars;
  - every number in the questions that name a car is that car's fact or a sum of them;
  - the locked car never appears.

  Run by hand once.

## Review Focus

1. **The balance changes between writing and racing** (a car bought, or points spent): a set written ahead may mention a car that's now locked again, but the student has already seen its facts. Accepted.
2. **Claude misquoting a fact** ("the P1's 950 hp"): the eval checks it, and the blind check may catch a wrong answer, but nothing on the server checks a quoted number. Decide whether that's acceptable for a family app.
3. **A garage file changed by hand** (a car removed, or a price changed): `in_reach` reads the garage loaded at startup, as the Showroom does.
4. **No goals:** car questions follow the same "how things work" rule.
5. **Prompt length:** at most 4 cars, about 400 characters.
