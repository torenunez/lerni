# v3.32: a friendlier race

**Goal:** The race reads each question by itself, one Sound switch covers the whole app, an honest bonus try is scored kindly and cheered, and a wrong answer doesn't always start with "Not this time".

**Why (2026-10-29, the admin watching the student race):**
- tapping the card to hear a question is one step too many;
- the Sound switch is only on the race screen, and Chat's spoken answers ignore it;
- honest bonus answers got low points and always the sputter;
- every wrong answer opens with "Not this time".

**The admin's decisions (2026-10-29):**
- ships as v3.32; stable ids, backups, one Chat, and probes each move down one (v3.33–v3.36);
- one Sound switch for the whole app, in the top bar;
- the gentler grade below, with the sound chosen by the score: more cheering, never a sputter for a bonus.

**Not in this version:** reading the bonus's graded line aloud; new sounds for multiple-choice answers (a wrong one still sputters).

## Changes

### 1. Questions read themselves (`web/race.js`, `web/games.py`)

- With sound on, each new question (the bonus too) is read once when it appears, as a tap does today. A tap reads it again.
- The track carries `data-ask="1"` when the card shows a question to answer. `race.js` presses the hidden reader once per race and step, the same way it says the right answer after a wrong one.
- Answering stops the reading (as today). Reading a question never holds Next.
- The Next tap unlocks the player too, like Race! and the answer taps, so the iPad lets the next question play.

### 2. One Sound switch, everywhere (`web/main.py`, `web/app.py`, `web/talk.js`, `web/race.js`)

Mockup, the top bar at phone width:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `☰  Games            ⭐ 120` | `☰  Games       ⭐ 120   🔊` |
| (in the race, under the track: `🔊 Sound: on`) | (gone from the race) |

- **The button:** 🔊 or 🔇 in the header on every screen, 44px tall, `aria-label` "Sound on" or "Sound off". It keeps today's `lerni-sound` setting on the device, so nobody's choice changes.
- **What it covers:** race sounds, reading questions and right answers, and Chat's spoken answers. With sound off, Chat still shows the answer as text, and the voice it got is dropped in the page.
- **Where the code lives:** the switch's label and tap move from `race.js` to `talk.js`, which every screen loads; `window.lerniSound()` tells `race.js` whether sound is on. Turning it off stops anything playing, as today.

### 3. A kinder bonus grade (`race.py`, `scripts/eval_bonus.py`)

- **The grader prompt:**
  - a key idea counts when it's said loosely, in everyday words, or half right;
  - "tried" is any real attempt about the question;
  - when unsure between two tiers, choose the higher.
- **Points:** "tried" goes from 2 to 5 ⭐ (`BONUS_POINTS`). Nailed 15, partly 7, none and care 0, as today.
- **The car:** unchanged. Tried still moves a small step forward.
- **The eval:** two new cases, a loose answer in simple words graded "partly", and an on-topic try graded at least "tried". The cheating case stays.

### 4. The sound follows the score (`web/games.py`, `web/race.js`)

- The server chooses each step's sound and puts it on the track as `data-sound`; `race.js` plays it once, instead of guessing from the count of right answers.

| What happened | Sound |
|---|---|
| A right answer | roar |
| A wrong answer (multiple choice) | sputter |
| Bonus nailed | zoom (a new clip; the roar until it's there) |
| Bonus partly | roar |
| Bonus tried | a short, soft roar |
| Bonus none, or care | nothing |
| The last answer | finish, as today |

- **The new clip:** `zoom.mp3` goes beside the others in `<data>/sounds/` (Pixabay, never committed). The admin picks it from the clips already downloaded. If it's missing, the roar plays.

### 5. Wrong answers open differently (`race.py`, `web/games.py`)

- `miss_opener(race, number) -> str` picks from: "Good try!", "Tricky one!", "Nice guess!", "Not quite!", "Not this time.", and "Good thinking!".
- **Never twice in a row:** the first miss starts at a place set by the race id, and each later miss moves on one, so two misses in a race never share an opener.
- **Screen and voice match:** the card, the last-question note, and `read_why` all use it: "Tricky one! It's **Saturn**. …".
