# v3.26 The student's page, tidied: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The educator's view of the student opens on one clear job: suggest a new goal. Today the top shows a dense line, "⭐ 140 · cars: … This week: Fractions 7/10 · …", which is hard to read and distracting. The admin wants it calmer: a heading, then the box, then racing shown as something you can read at a glance, then the lists, with the map at the bottom.

**The admin's decisions (2026-10-24):**
- the layout, top to bottom: "Suggest a new goal", then "Racing this week", then Goals and Interests, then the map at the very bottom;
- racing shown as option A from the mockups: a bar per goal, with ⭐ and the car they drive as two small chips;
- shipped as v3.26, ahead of probes (now v3.27).

**Architecture (`web/educator.py`, `web/games.py`, `web/app.py`):** the page, top to bottom:
1. **The picker,** hidden with one student, as in v3.25.
2. **"Suggest a new goal"** as a heading, then the box (📎, Send), Lerni's reply, the ticked suggestions, **Add N to the map**, and the status line.
3. **"Racing this week":**
   - **Each goal raced in the last 7 days** gets a row: its name, a bar filled right/asked, and "7 of 10". Most recently raced first, as `ResultsLog.week` returns them.
   - **Then two chips:** "⭐ 140" and "🚗 <the car they drive>". The car is `Points.car`, named from the garage; a car gone from the garage file shows no chip.
   - **No races this week:** the rows are replaced by one line, "No races this week."
   - **Built as HTML** by `student_progress(...)` in `games.py`, which replaces `student_points`. Every name is HTML-escaped, and colours use the app's CSS variables, so it reads in dark mode.
   - **Roles:** the same check as today (`map_owner`); anyone else gets "".
4. **The list** (`mental_list(…, folded=True)`): the add form, then Goals and Interests folded, Removed, and Older interests.
5. **"Conversation with Lerni",** folded.
6. **The MentalMap picture,** last.

**Tech Stack:** Gradio 6.30, pytest.

**Spec:** [spec 06](specs/06-educator-chat.md) ("What the educator sees") and the educator PRD's "See the map" story, both updated here.

## Global Constraints

- **Rule 5:** unchanged. The bars are read-only, and suggestions still need the Add tap.
- **Rule 8:** no student name or goal from the real map in code, tests, or docs. The mockups' names are made up.
- **Rule 9:** checked at phone size as an educator, right after a restart, with both MentalMap lists. Fields stay at 16 px.
- **Tests:** `test_web_roles.py`'s points check becomes a check of `student_progress`:
  - an educator sees ⭐, the car's name, and "3 of 5" for a raced goal;
  - another viewer gets "";
  - a goal name with `<` is escaped.

## Review Focus

1. **A student with no races and the starter car:** "No races this week" and both chips; nothing breaks.
2. **Many goals raced in one week** (10 or more): the card stays readable at phone width, with long names wrapped, not cut.
3. **Dark mode:** the bars and chips stay visible.
4. **A picture tap** still opens the item in the list now that the picture sits below it.
5. **Picking another student** (with two or more) redraws the card for them.
