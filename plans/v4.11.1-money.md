# v4.11.1: money in races

**Goal:** coins and bills drawn by code, counted and totaled, then making change, as a second picture track beside blocks ([spec 11](specs/11-new-ways-to-answer.md#picture-math)). Rulers follow in v4.11.2.

## The admin's calls (2026-11-17)

- **Money:** coins (penny, nickel, dime, quarter), then $1, $5, $20, and $100 bills, then both together ($1.35); totals, then making change.
- **Real coins, drawn bills,** each labeled.
- **One game:** like blocks, money comes as a typed question inside races (v4.11).

## My calls

- **Money is a second track of the picture kind,** not a kind of its own. A race's picture slots go to the track that needs it most: its miss rate over its last 10 answers, plus a little while its level isn't the top one; a tie goes to the one practiced least recently. As kinds of their own, blocks, money, and rulers would take every slot of a race.
- **Available** for a player with a goal in the Math area, as blocks are.
- **Answers are numbers,** on the number keyboard (with a decimal point for dollars):
  - cents as a whole number ("35");
  - dollars as "1.35", "$1.35", "1.5", ".65", or "65¢"; a whole dollar amount may be "7" or "7.00".
- **Money is counted in whole cents in code,** never as decimals, so $0.10 + $0.20 is exactly 30 cents.
- **Levels** (moving every 5 answers, as blocks do):
  1. one coin, named: "This is a dime. How many cents is it worth?";
  2. a few coins of one kind: "How many cents?";
  3. mixed coins, up to 6: "How many cents in all?";
  4. bills only: "How many dollars?";
  5. bills and coins: "How much money? Write it like 1.35";
  6. making change: "You pay with $5 for a $3.25 toy car. How much change?" (the bill and the price drawn; the toy from an interest's emoji).
- **Coins are real photos** (the admin's call): public-domain United States Mint images of the heads side, each drawn at its real diameter relative to the others (the admin's call: dime 17.91 mm, penny 19.05 mm, nickel 21.21 mm, quarter 24.26 mm, so the dime is smallest; a quarter about 72 px wide), each labeled with its name (penny, nickel, dime, quarter), never its value; the Mint's credit line under money questions. Kept in the repo, `web/coins/` (sources in its `CREDITS.md`).
- **Bills are drawn** (the admin's call): green rectangles labeled $1, $5, $20, $100. Color images of real bills are allowed only under conditions (under ¾ size, one side, and the files used to make them deleted after use), which don't fit an app that keeps them.
- **After an answer:** how it adds up ("2 quarters and 1 dime is 60¢").

## Code

- **`pictures.py`** (from `blocks.py`): `PicturesDesk`, the picture kind's source, with tracks (`blocks`, `money`). `pictures/<user>.json` holds each track's level and last few answers (numbers only); an existing `blocks/<user>.json` is read as the blocks track once, so no level is lost.
- **`money.py`** (new): coins and bills, `question(level, rng, emoji)`, and `cents_typed(text) -> int | None`.
- **`blocks.py`:** only its questions stay (`question`, `emoji_for`, `typed_number`); the store and desk move to `pictures.py`.
- **`web/games.py`:** `picture_html` draws coins and bills too; `race.js` gives money the decimal keyboard (`inputmode="decimal"`).
- **Rule 6:** "picture levels (numbers)" in place of "blocks levels".

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **Happy path:** each money level's answer matches its drawing, in cents, and the coins' drawn widths follow their real diameters; `cents_typed` reads "1.35" and "$1.35" as 135 cents, and refuses "1.3.5".
- **Tracks:** the track missed most gets the picture slot; an old blocks file keeps its level.
- **Guarantee:** a typed amount never reaches disk (as for blocks).

## Checks

At phone size: the decimal keyboard on money, coins readable at phone width, a 6-coin picture fitting one screen.

## Deploying

- **Safe while in use:** pull, reinstall, restart. A restart clears any race in progress.
- **Data:** `pictures/` is made on the first picture answer; the old `blocks/` file is read once and left in place.
- **Rolling back to v4.11:** blocks go back to `blocks/`, missing answers given since; money questions stop.
