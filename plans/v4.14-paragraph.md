# v4.14: a paragraph

**Goal:** a first paragraph inside the game. One writing race a day: one prompt, a 5-item checklist, one revision, the same cars and ⭐ ([spec 11](specs/11-new-ways-to-answer.md#a-paragraph), [to-do](../docs/todo.md#upcoming-versions)).

## The admin's calls (2026-11-18)

- **A writing race:** the whole race is one paragraph. Each checklist item marked ✓ counts as a right answer and moves the car. One revision can turn a "not yet" into ✓.
- **Once a day:** the race right after the day's first reading race (usually the 3rd race).

## Already decided (spec 11)

- **Typed only:** no voice, since speaking would skip spelling and punctuation.
- **Two kinds of prompt:** a response to the passage just read, or a creative one from the student's interests.
- **The checklist, shown before writing:**
  1. a first sentence that says what it's about;
  2. 2–3 sentences with details;
  3. at least 2 describing words;
  4. capitals and periods;
  5. a last sentence that wraps up.
- **Feedback:** ✓ or "not yet" per item, one thing done well, and one thing to try. After the revision, the grade names what got better.
- **Misspelled words** are suggested to the educator as spelling words, added only on their tap.

## My calls

- **The prompts come from the reading call, at no extra cost:** the passage's JSON gains two short prompts, one response and one creative. Writing races alternate between the two kinds (the response first). With no reading race today, there's no writing race; the next day tries again.
- **Code grades what it can:**
  - **capitals and periods:** each sentence starts with a capital and ends with `.`, `!` or `?`; `i` alone counts as a miss;
  - **details:** needs 4 or more sentences, plus Claude's yes;
  - **describing words:** Claude counts them, and code checks for at least 2.
- **Claude (Haiku) grades the rest in one call:**
  - the first sentence, the details and the wrap-up (yes or no each);
  - the describing-word count;
  - one line on what went well and one thing to try;
  - misspelled words, as their right spellings;
  - a care flag, as the bonus has.
  
  It gets the prompt, the passage when there is one, and the paragraph. A revision costs one more call. That's at most 2 grading calls a day.
- **The flow:**
  1. The student writes and taps Check.
  2. The checklist marks ✓ or not yet, with the two lines.
  3. If every item is ✓, the race finishes. Otherwise there are two buttons: **Try again** (the paragraph stays in the field to edit) and **Done**.
  4. The final checklist moves the cars: 5 steps, one per item, as 5 answers would. The finish and ⭐ work as in any race.
- **The passage stays folded above** a response prompt (the v4.13 card). It's kept only in memory between the two races, so after a restart the prompt is creative instead.
- **A paragraph is up to 600 characters** (about 100 words). The field has autocorrect, suggestions, capitals and spell check off, at 16px.
- **A worry:** the caring words alone, as in the bonus, with no checklist. The paragraph goes to the conversation log. No ⭐ is lost: the race finishes with the checklist as it stood.
- **Suggested spelling words:**
  - up to 5 a paragraph, letters only, 3 or more letters;
  - only words not already in practice;
  - kept in the spelling file as `suggested` (the right spelling and the day), up to 15, each dropped after 14 days;
  - the educator's Spelling fold shows them as "Suggested from writing (tick to add)".
- **The educator sees,** in the Reading fold (renamed **Reading and writing**): paragraphs written in 7 days, ✓ per checklist item, and revisions that got better. `lerni stats` shows the same.

## Rule 6

The additions:
- the two writing prompts, held with the passage written ahead (deleted when their day ends);
- writing counts (numbers, and ✓ per checklist item);
- suggested spelling words (the right spellings, never as typed).

The paragraph itself goes only to the conversation log. The results log keeps one row per checklist item (`kind: write`, `label: first`, right or not).

## Code

- **`writing.py`** (new, standard library):
  - `CHECKLIST` (the 5 items' ids and names);
  - `capitals_ok(text)` and `sentences(text)`;
  - `GRADE_SCHEMA` and `GRADE_SYSTEM`;
  - `WritingStore` (`writing/<user>.json`: today's count, the next kind, 7-day tallies);
  - `WritingDesk`:
    - `due(username, day, read_today)`;
    - `task(username, day)`: the prompt and the passage;
    - `grade(...)`: a `Graded` with the checklist, the two lines, misspellings and care;
    - `finished(...)`;
    - `summary(...)`.
- **`reading.py`:**
  - `READ_SCHEMA` gains `respond` and `create`;
  - `ReadingDesk` keeps today's raced passage and its prompts in memory for the writing desk.
- **`race.py`:**
  - `Race.paragraph` (the task, the draft's checklist, whether it was revised);
  - `RaceDesk._writing_race`;
  - `RaceDesk.paragraph_answer(username, race_id, text, final)`: grades, and moves the cars for each item once it's final;
  - `_log` writes the 5 `write` rows.
- **`spelling.py`:** `suggest(username, words, day)` and `add_suggested(username, words)`; `summary` returns `suggested`.
- **`web/games.py`, `race.js`, CSS:**
  - the writing dock: the prompt, the checklist card, a 5-line field, **Check** / **Try again** / **Done**;
  - the passage card reused.
- **`web/spelling.py`:** the suggestions list and **Add ticked**.
- **`web/reading.py`:** the fold's name and the writing lines.
- **`stats.py`:** `TYPED_NAMES` gains `"write": "Writing"`; `commands/stats.py` prints the writing lines.
- **The meter:** `writing-grade`.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **Happy path:** a writing race through fakes:
  - due after a reading race;
  - a draft with 3 ✓;
  - Try again with 5 ✓;
  - the cars move 5 right steps;
  - the counts saved;
  - a suggested word reaches the educator's list and is added on a tap.
- **Safety:** rule 6. After a writing race, the paragraph's words (a marker) are nowhere on disk but the conversation log.
- **`capitals_ok`:** one right and one wrong paragraph, in the same test as the happy path.
- **CI:** the writing race test is marked `ci`.

## Evals (real calls, run by hand)

**`scripts/eval_writing.py`, 3 cases:**
- a good paragraph: every Claude item is ✓;
- a list of facts with no wrap-up: wrap-up not yet, and "try" names it;
- misspellings ("becuase", "frend"): they come back as the right spellings.
