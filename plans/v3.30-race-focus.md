# v3.30 A focused race: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** During a race the screen shows only the race. A wrong answer's right answer is said out loud, in bigger text.

**Why (2026-10-27, the admin watching the student race):**
- the garage (My cars and the Showroom) sits right under the race and distracts;
- the right answer after a wrong one is good, but hard to read: the text is small, and the garage takes the space;
- it should also be said out loud.

**The admin's decisions (2026-10-27):**
- the garage shows only when not racing;
- a wrong answer's right answer is spoken by itself, and turning sound off silences it too;
- this ships as v3.30, before One Chat (now v3.31, its plan on `feat/v3.30-one-chat`) and probes (v3.32);
- the race takes the top third of the screen, the question, answers, and Next the rest;
- no speaker buttons: tapping the question card reads it, tapping the right-answer card reads that;
- one switch, "🔊 Sound: on/off", for all sound (engine sounds, the spoken right answer, and reading on tap); it replaces v3.29's 🔇 and Read aloud switches. With sound on, the right answer is spoken by itself; a question is read when tapped.

**Architecture:**
- **The garage between races (`web/games.py`):**
  - My cars and the Showroom move into one `gr.Column` (`garage_box`).
  - `screen()` shows it only when no race is running (before the first, and after the finish).
  - The hero (your car and next goal) already hides during a race.
- **Saying the right answer (`race.py`):** `RaceDesk.read_why(username, race_id, number) -> bytes | None` speaks "Not this time. It's ⟨right answer⟩. ⟨why⟩".
  - **Only allowed:** for the question just answered, and only if that answer was wrong (`race.marks[number] is False`, `number == race.answered - 1`).
  - **Shared with read-aloud:** the same voice, the same memory-only cache (keyed `("why", number)`), and the same check after speaking that the race hasn't moved on.
- **The screen:**
  - **When the why shows,** whether on the card mid-race or in the note after the last question, the track carries `data-why="N"`, and a tap on it reads the why.
  - **The hidden reader (`on_speak`)** reads the why when one is showing (a `gr.State` of its number, set by `screen()`), else the question.
  - **`race.js`:** when `data-why` is set and sound is on, it presses the hidden reader once per race and step. The answer tap already unlocked the player.
- **A third for the race (`web/app.py`):** the track's picture is capped at `30dvh` (scaled down, keeping its shape, centred), with the two switches under it. The question, answers or right answer, and Next take the rest.
- **Tap to hear, one switch (`web/games.py`, `web/race.js`):**
  - **No buttons:** the 🔊 button and the Read aloud switch go. The question card shows "Question 2 of 5 · tap to hear".
  - **A tap on the card** (the question, or the right answer) presses a hidden reader, if sound is on.
  - **The one switch,** "🔊 Sound: on/off", keeps the `lerni-sound` setting. Off silences the engine sounds, the spoken right answer, and reading on tap. The old `lerni-read` setting is ignored.
- **Bigger text (`web/app.py`):**
  - the question goes from 18 px to 22 px;
  - the why card's right answer and explanation to 20 px;
  - the end-of-race note to 18 px;
  - the choice buttons stay as they are.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+, Gradio 6.30, vanilla JS, pytest.

**Spec:** [spec 09b](specs/09b-harder-races.md) ("Read aloud", and the race screen).

## Global Constraints

- **Rule 6:** spoken text and audio stay in memory only.
- **Rule 9:**
  - checked at phone size;
  - the admin checks sound on the iPad.
- **Tests (rule 4):** one in `test_race.py`, `test_a_wrong_answer_is_said_out_loud_and_only_then`:
  - after a wrong answer, `read_why` speaks the right answer and why;
  - after a right answer it gives None;
  - so does a number that isn't the last answer.

## Review Focus

1. **A wrong answer on the last question:** the race ends, the garage comes back, and the why in the note is still spoken once.
2. **A quick Next while the why is still being spoken:** the reading stops, and Read aloud (if on) reads the next question.
3. **Sound off:** nothing is spoken, not even on a tap; the switch says so.
4. **The bonus's grade line:** not spoken by itself (it isn't a wrong multiple-choice answer).
5. **The garage after Race again:** hidden at once, with no flash of tiles.
