# v4.6.2: race questions answered from words alone

**Goal:** no race question needs a picture or a sound the screen doesn't show.

## Why (the admin, 2026-11-12)

- **Music questions felt too frequent,** and the student missed many of them.
- **They're hard for the wrong reason.** The student hears no notes and sees no staff, yet questions asked where a note sits on the staff, or described what a note looks like ("a hollow note with a stem").
- **The misses fed the schedule.** Each miss keeps a goal in the 1-day box, so the same kind of question came back daily.

## Checked and set aside

- **Importance per goal** (Less / Normal / More): offered; the admin chose the rule only for now.

## Change

- **`QUIZ_SYSTEM`** gains one rule: every question is answered from words alone; never ask the student to read, picture, or hear something (a note's place on a staff, a note's look, a tune); ask about the idea in words instead.
- **Each goal takes its own turn** (`pick_goals`, the admin's call after the simulation): before, each area got one turn per set, so a 2-goal area's goals came up far more often than an 8-goal area's. Now goals go in schedule order (due first, then least practiced), at most 2 from one area (`AREA_CAP`), so sets still mix subjects. A 14-day simulation showed a small shift, since misses drive most of it; it's fairer, not the main fix.
- **No data changes.** Questions already held for today still race; new writes follow the rule.

## Checks

- **Eval** (`eval_race.py words`, real calls, run by hand): a set on two music goals.
  - The old prompt fails: 7 questions about where notes sit on the staff.
  - The new prompt passes: the facts are given in words ("the line notes are E, G, B, D, F"), and no question asks what a note looks or sounds like.
- **Still the educator's call:** a goal about the staff itself still names lines and spaces in words. The educator rewords its notes in Maps (for example, "the music alphabet A to G") or removes it; v4.7's guidance can say "no staff questions".
- **Tests:** the goal-picking test now checks the cap (three Math goals never raced: only 2 are picked); it fails on the old area turns. The prompt rule is covered by the eval.

## Deploying

- **Safe while in use:** pull, reinstall, restart. A restart clears open conversations; today's held questions are kept (v4.6.1).
