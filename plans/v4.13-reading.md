# v4.13: reading races

**Goal:** reading comprehension inside the game: a passage above the track, and a race whose five questions are all about it ([spec 11](specs/11-new-ways-to-answer.md#reading), [to-do](../docs/todo.md#upcoming-versions)).

## The admin's calls (2026-11-17)

- **A reading race:** one passage sits above the track and all 5 questions are about it. Same cars, same ⭐. Normal races are unchanged.
- **Three ways to answer, in every reading race:**
  - multiple choice (tap);
  - sentences put in time order (tap each in turn);
  - open answers, **talked out loud or typed**.
- **Sources:** the educator's picks from the shortlist (all 27 ticked): narrative, informational, and opinion. They're kept in the private data folder, never in the repo. The repo carries only a generic example: two Aesop fables, plus opinion pieces written about the student's interests.
- **Facts from the page:** for the government pages (NOAA, the Park Service, NASA, the Department of Energy), Lerni fetches the page when it writes, and Claude retells only what the page says. Stories are retold from what Claude knows.

## My calls

- **Cadence:** a day's 2nd race is a reading race, then every 3rd (2, 5, 8…), when a passage is ready. If none is ready, it's a normal race, and the next race tries again.
- **Kinds take turns:** narrative → informational → opinion. Within a kind, the source least recently read comes first.
- **The five questions, the same shape for every kind** (choice, open, choice, order, open):

  | # | Narrative | Informational | Opinion |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | What kind of text? 📖 Story · 🔬 Facts · 💬 Opinion (tap) | same | same |
  | 2 | Why? (open, a few words) | same | same |
  | 3 | Who, where, when (tap); from level 4, why or how | The main idea (tap) | The author's opinion (tap) |
  | 4 | What happened, in time order (order) | The facts or steps in the order the passage gives them (order) | Opinion, reasons, wrap-up in the order the author gives them (order) |
  | 5 | The main character in 2 describing words (open) | Two details that support the main idea (open) | A reason the author gives, and do you agree? (open) |

- **The reading level is Lerni's own,** from 1 to 6, per student. It moves like race levels: every 5 answers (one reading race), up at 4+ right, down at 2 or fewer. Each level has a target, checked by a readability formula in code (Flesch–Kincaid grade):

  | Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Target grade | 1.5 | 2.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 |
  | About this many words | 80 | 105 | 130 | 155 | 180 | 205 |
  | Sentences to put in order | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |

  A passage measuring more than 1 grade above its target is rewritten once. If it's still too hard, it's dropped and the race is a normal one.
- **Grading:**
  - **choices and order:** graded by code; order counts only when the whole order is right;
  - **open answers:** graded by Claude (Haiku). It gets the passage, the question, and the key ideas, and says right or not yet, one short line, how many describing words the answer used, and a care flag (as the bonus has).
- **The adjective nudge (spec 11):** when an open answer names a thing with no describing word ("a shark"), it gets one nudge ("What kind of shark?") and one more try. The second answer is graded either way.
- **Checks before a passage is raced:**
  - JSON shape, the order sentences found in the passage, and the readability check, all in code;
  - a blind Claude check answers the choice questions from the passage alone; any disagreement drops the passage (as `_verify` does for races).
- **The passage is not read aloud.** It's reading practice. The questions still are, as now.
- **On screen:**
  - the passage card has its title, but no kind label (question 1 asks the kind);
  - it can fold away while the keyboard is up;
  - open answers use the bonus's talk-or-type dock; order uses tap cards in the typed dock;
  - a reading race has no bonus, and no spelling or picture slots.
- **Written ahead:** one passage per student, in the prefill thread, once a reading race is next. It's kept on disk until it's raced or its day ends (like race questions written ahead).
- **Fetching:** only sources marked `fetch` (https `.gov` pages), with a 10-second timeout. The text is pulled out with `html.parser`, capped at 8,000 characters, and kept in memory only. A fetch that fails skips that source this time.
- **The educator sees:** a read-only Reading fold showing the level, right per question type and per kind over 7 days, and "open answers with describing words: N of M". `lerni stats` shows the same lines.

## Rule 6

The additions:
- the reading sources the educator picked (titles, links, how to use them);
- reading passages written ahead and not yet raced (deleted when their day ends);
- reading levels and counts (numbers, and the source titles read).

Fetched pages are never written to disk. The results log keeps labels and numbers only (`read`, `narrative:order`, the number of describing words), never what was said or typed.

## Code

- **`reading.py`** (new, standard library):
  - `Source` and `Passage`;
  - `fk_grade(text)` and `syllables(word)`;
  - `ReadingStore` (`reading/sources.json`, `reading/<user>.json`, `reading/ahead/<user>.json`);
  - `fetch_text(url)`;
  - `write_passage` and `check_passage`;
  - `ReadingSource`, a `TypedSource` for order and open slots;
  - `grade_open`;
  - `import_sources(csv)`.
- **`race.py`:**
  - `Race.passage`;
  - `Question.reading` (a label; no goal, so no concepts, boxes, or bonus);
  - `RaceDesk.start` makes a reading race when it's due;
  - `typed_answer` handles a nudge (`Checked.again`);
  - `_log` writes `kind: read`.
- **`web/games.py`, `race.js`, CSS:**
  - the passage card;
  - tap-to-order cards;
  - open answers through the talk-or-type dock.
- **`commands/`:** `lerni reading import <csv>`, which reads the Sheet's CSV, keeps the ticked rows, and sets `fetch` on `.gov` links.
- **`stats.py`:** the reading lines.
- **The educator:** a read-only Reading fold.
- **The meter:** each Claude call is named with `using`:
  - `reading-write` writes a passage;
  - `reading-check` is the blind check;
  - `reading-grade` grades an open answer.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **Happy path:**
  - `fk_grade` on two known texts, one easy and one hard, in the right order and range;
  - a reading race through fakes: the 5 slots in order, order graded by code, an open answer through a fake grader, and one nudge then a grade.
- **Safety:**
  - rule 6: after a reading race, nothing typed or said is on disk;
  - the checks drop a passage whose order sentences aren't in it.
- **CI:** the reading race test is marked `ci`.

## Evals (real calls, run by hand)

- **`scripts/eval_reading.py`, 3 cases:**
  - a passage at levels 1 and 4: within its readability band, and the blind check agrees;
  - open answers: a good one, a bare noun (nudged), and an off-topic one.

## Deploying

- **Safe while in use:** pull, reinstall, restart. A restart clears any race in progress.
- **Setup, once:** export the Sheet as CSV and run `lerni reading import <csv>` on production. Until then, reading races use the built-in example.
- **Network:** the server fetches `.gov` pages when it writes a passage.
