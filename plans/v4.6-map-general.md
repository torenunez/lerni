# v4.6: the map picture shows general interests only

**Goal:** a calmer MentalMap picture: only general interests and goals, each sized by how often it came up, so the big circles are what the student keeps coming back to.

**Why (the admin, 2026-11-11):** on a real map, kinds (a car model under cars) crowd the picture and every busy item hits the same "strong" size, so the picture can't show which interests are biggest.

## What changes

- **Only top-level items are drawn:** an interest that isn't a kind of anything, and every goal. Kinds stay in the list below the picture, unchanged.
- **Links to a kind draw to its general interest** (its top-level groups, if it's in more than one):
  - a link that ends up inside one group isn't drawn;
  - two links that end up on the same pair draw once, a bridge before a related line.
- **Size by days, on a growing scale:** a circle's area grows with the days it or any kind under it came up, all time (`group_days`). The radius is `9 + 4.5 × √days`, and a goal is sized by its own days the same way.
  - Never discussed: 11, as today.
  - The cap is 40, which comes at about 48 days, so one huge interest can't swamp the picture.
- **Unchanged:**
  - fading after 30 quiet days (the faint look) still shows what's recent;
  - a goal not discussed yet stays white;
  - a goal that backed off stays dashed;
  - tapping a circle lights its links and opens it in the list.

## The mockup

A made-up map: the same 14 items before, and 9 circles after (trains, dinosaurs and space absorb their kinds). It was shown to the admin on 2026-11-12, before any code.

## Code

- **`interests.py`:**
  - **`roots(m, entry_id) -> list[str]`:** the top-level groups above an item (itself if it's in none). It follows `parents`, and a stored loop never recurses forever.
  - **`picture_view(m) -> (entries, links)`:** the top-level entries and the links moved to their roots, as above.
- **`web/mapdraw.py`:**
  - `map_svg` draws `picture_view(m)`;
  - `radius(days)` replaces `RADII` and `strength` for size (`strength` stays for the list);
  - the layout and frame are unchanged.
- **`mapview.js`:** no change, since lines carry the drawn ids.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **The happy path, a made-up map with a kind under a group:**
  - the picture has no circle for the kind;
  - the kind's bridge is drawn from its group;
  - the group's circle is bigger than one with fewer days.
- **Safety (a stored loop):** `roots` on a kind-of loop returns without recursing forever.
