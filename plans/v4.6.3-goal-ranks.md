# v4.6.3: goal priorities and a fair share

**Goal:** each goal gets about its share of race questions, set by its priority and nudged by how the student is doing, instead of a goal missed often crowding out the rest.

## Why (the admin, 2026-11-14)

- **The boxes swing too far.** A goal missed lately comes back daily, and a mastered one every 14 days. That's up to 14× the questions, so two goals in a small area took about 19% of the races in a 14-day simulation.
- **Not every goal matters equally.** In the admin's words, Math and Language are the top goals and everything else comes after, with a third level possible later.
- **The 5:00 write-ahead failed two mornings in a row** (2026-11-13 and 14). The log said only `ResultError`, and nothing tried again, so those days had nothing held.

## What the educator sees (the mockup, 2026-11-14)

- **An area's heading in the goal list has a Priority dropdown:** High, Medium, or Low. Every goal in that area follows it, new ones too.
- **An open goal has a Priority dropdown** beside Area: "Math's (High)", High, Medium, or Low, with a Set button.
  - A goal with its own priority shows it as a small tag in the list.
  - Choosing the area's option returns the goal to following its area.
- **The defaults:** areas start at Medium, and goals start by following their area. The admin sets Math and Language to High with two taps.
- **The same list** serves an independent student's own map.
- **Priorities are saved only from a person's tap** (rule 5).

## The admin's calls

- **Weights:** High counts 2×, Medium 1×, and Low 0.5×.
- **The words:** High, Medium, and Low, not the admin's work jargon (P1, P2, P3), so other educators read them easily.
- **A priority per area, with a per-goal override** (not per goal only).
- **The two morning fixes** join this version.

## Code

- **`interests.py`:**
  - `Entry.rank` (0 = follows its area, else 1 High, 2 Medium, 3 Low);
  - `InterestMap.area_ranks` (area name in lowercase → 1–3);
  - `rank_of(m, entry)` returns the goal's own rank, else its area's, else 2;
  - `set_rank(m, entry_id, rank)`;
  - `set_area_rank(m, area, rank)`;
  - a hand-edited rank outside 1–3 reads as "follows"; the screens say High, Medium, Low (`RANK_NAMES`).
- **`results.py`:** `tally_since(username, since)` returns (asked, right) by goal id. It replaces `asked_since`.
- **`race.py`, `pick_goals`, the fair share:**
  - each goal's weight = `RANK_WEIGHT[rank]` (High 2, Medium 1, Low 0.5) × how it's going over the last 14 days:
    - 1.5 when fewer than 60% were right (at least 3 answers);
    - 0.5 when at least 90% were right (at least 4 answers);
    - otherwise 1;
  - a set takes the goals with the lowest "questions in 14 days ÷ weight": a goal never asked comes first, and ties go to the least asked, then the id;
  - at most 2 goals from one area (`AREA_CAP`, from v4.6.2);
  - the boxes still move with answers, but no longer pick goals. Missed ideas keep their own review schedule.
- **`web/mentallist.py`:**
  - the area heading's dropdown, saved when changed;
  - the open goal's Priority dropdown with Set;
  - the tag on a goal that has its own priority.
- **`web/app.py`, the 5:00 run:** runs again every 30 minutes until 9:00 (`AHEAD_TRIES`) while something failed, and stops once everything is written. It's safe to repeat:
  - the morning plan skips once it ran today. A failed call now counts as a try, not a run (`SuggestionStore.failed`), and is given up after 3 tries a day (`MORNING_TRIES`). Before, a failure was stamped as the day's run, so nothing tried again;
  - the write-ahead skips while enough questions are held (`RaceDesk.ready`);
  - so a retry only redoes what failed.
- **`adapters/claude_code.py`:** a failed call logs the error's reason and status, labels and numbers only, never its text. Example: `ResultError (api_error, 529)`.

## Tests (fakes only, minimal)

- **The happy path:**
  - with Math at High and the rest at Medium, the 14-day share of a fake run tracks the weights;
  - a goal's own priority overrides its area's.
- **Regressions:**
  - a 5:00 run whose first try fails is tried again;
  - a failed call's log line carries its reason and status and none of its text.
