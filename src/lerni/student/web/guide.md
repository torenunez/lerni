## Welcome 👋

1. **Add your family** in **Students**. *Supervised* for a student you sit beside; *independent* for anyone planning their own learning.
2. **Make a plan** in **Learning plans**. Start from the cars or sharks example, or paste rough notes and let Claude organize them.
3. **Fill in one activity card:** a short explanation, a question, 2–3 choices, the answer, and a hint.
4. **Try it together**, then change one thing.

Independent learners can also **Ask** Lerni anything, by text.

Keep names and personal details out of plans.
